Professional Stage Lighting Manufacturer in Guangzhou, China

RiGeBa Lighting is the trading name of GuangZhou GeLiang Lighting Technology Co.,Ltd., a stage lighting manufacturer operating an 8,000 m² factory in Huadu District, Guangzhou since 2010. We design, build and export 632 lighting models to rental houses, distributors and venues in more than 60 countries.

What RiGeBa manufactures

Stage lighting built in our own factory

Every category below is assembled, tested and packed on our own floor — we are the manufacturer, not a trading company.

Moving Head Lights 64 models Beam, spot, wash and hybrid fixtures plus multi-head bars for touring rigs and rental houses. Pixel & Effects 81 models Pixel-mapped bars, battens and effect fixtures for audience looks and immersive shows. LED Profile Spots 33 models Framing-light profile fixtures with interchangeable lenses for theatre and stage. LED PAR & Uplights 62 models Flat PAR fixtures for washes, uplighting and architectural installations. Stage Lasers 37 models Aerial and beam lasers for clubs, festivals and event productions. LED Gobo & Logo Projectors 37 models Gobo and logo projectors for branding and scenography. LED Kinetic Winch & Ball 19 models Kinetic winches and lifting balls for vertical movement over a stage. Manufacturing capabilities

From SMT to packing, under one roof

R&D

Optical, mechanical and firmware design in-house; new models are built on our own tooling.

SMT

Surface-mount lines for driver and control boards.

PCB assembly

Board population, firmware programming and in-circuit test.

Mechanical assembly

Sheet-metal and CNC machining for yokes, bases and housings.

Optical assembly

Lens and optic fitting, focus calibration and beam alignment.

Aging test

Fixtures run under load before they are allowed to pack.

QC

Inspection at each stage, with function and DMX checks on every unit.

Packaging & loading

Foam-fitted flight cases, carton packing and container loading.

Questions buyers ask

Stage lighting manufacturer FAQ

Is RiGeBa a stage lighting manufacturer or a trading company?

We are the manufacturer. RiGeBa Lighting is the brand of GuangZhou GeLiang Lighting Technology Co.,Ltd., which owns an 8,000 m² factory in Huadu District, Guangzhou. Design, SMT, assembly, aging test, QC and packing all happen on our own floor.

Where is RiGeBa Lighting located?

No. 21, Lingdong Road, Xiuquan Street, Huadu District, Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. The factory and the office are at the same address.

Does RiGeBa provide OEM and ODM service?

Yes. We manufacture under customer brands as well as our own, covering private label, custom colours, logos, firmware and packaging. See the OEM / ODM page for the process.

What stage lighting products do you manufacture?

632 models across 12 categories: moving heads (beam, spot, wash, hybrid and multi-head bars), pixel and effects, LED profile spots, LED PAR, stage lasers, gob projectors, DMX controllers and splitters, theatre lights, effect machines, kinetic winches, dance floors and aluminum truss.

Can RiGeBa customise stage lighting?

Yes. Colour temperature, beam angle, housing finish, logo and packaging can be adapted, and we can develop a new model from a reference sample. MOQ applies per project.

Do you manufacture IP65 outdoor moving heads?

Yes. Our outdoor range includes IP65-rated beam and wash moving heads for permanent outdoor stages, festivals and coastal installations.

How many countries do you export to?

More than 60 countries, through lighting rental companies, distributors, theatres, clubs, event production companies and private-label brands.

How long has RiGeBa been manufacturing?

Since 2010. The company is managed to ISO 9001:2015.

Need a quotation or a sample?

Send us your fixture list and the delivery window. We reply with an EXW quotation, the OEM options and the lead time.
