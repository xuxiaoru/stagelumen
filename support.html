FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the typical lead time?

Standard products typically ship within 7-15 days from order confirmation. OEM and custom projects require additional prototyping and approval time.

Do you provide DMX personality files?

Yes. We provide DMX charts and fixture personality files for major lighting consoles. Contact support for specific console formats.

Can I request a sample before bulk order?

Yes, sample orders are available for evaluation. Sample pricing and lead time depend on the specific product and destination.

What certifications do your products have?

Our standard products carry CE and RoHS. FCC, UL, RCM, PSE and other certifications are available on request for qualified orders.

How do I get technical support?

Use the contact form or email support@stagelumen.com with your product model, purchase reference and a description of the issue.

Need Technical Support?

Our team is here to help with DMX configuration, troubleshooting and spare parts.
