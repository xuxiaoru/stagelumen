About StageLumen

Professional Stage Lighting, Engineered for Global Production

Who We Are

StageLumen is a professional stage lighting manufacturer focused on LED moving heads, pixel effects, theatre fixtures, outdoor lighting and OEM/ODM services for global buyers.

What We Build

We develop and manufacture reliable, professional-grade lighting fixtures for rental companies, distributors, event production teams, venues and entertainment professionals.

Who We Serve

Our customers include lighting rental companies, distributors, theatres, clubs, churches, event production companies and private-label brands across 60+ countries.

Engineering

Our R&D team works on optical, mechanical, electronic and firmware design to develop fixtures that meet real-world production demands.

Quality Control

Every product goes through component inspection, assembly, aging test, movement test and final QC before packing and shipment.

Export & OEM/ODM

We support FOB, CIF and DDP shipping terms, and offer flexible product customization, branding and private-label production for qualified partners.

Our Journey

15 years, three factories, one mission

Founded in Guangzhou

Started with 6 engineers and a 1,200 m² workshop, focusing on PAR cans for export.

2010

First 100W LED Series

Expanded into moving heads and DMX consoles; shipped our first 5,000-unit container.

2013

ISO 9001 + CE Certification

Opened a second 3,000 m² line, dedicated to OEM production for European brands.

2016

Outdoor IP65 Range

R&D team grew to 40 engineers. Released weatherproof outdoor series for festivals.

2019

Smart Lighting & Lasers

Released pixel-mappable bars and 30W ILDA lasers. Set up US/EU logistics partners.

2022

8,000 m² Smart Factory

Moved into current facility with SMT lines, automated aging chambers and AGV packing.
