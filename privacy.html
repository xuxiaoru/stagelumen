Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

1. Who We Are

StageLumen Lighting Co., Ltd. ("we", "us", "our") operates the website stagelumen.com (the "Site"). We are a manufacturer of professional stage lighting based in Guangzhou, China.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when submitting a quote request, downloading resources, subscribing to our newsletter, or contacting sales. This may include: name, company, country, email, phone number, project details.

We also automatically collect device, browser and usage data through cookies and analytics tools (Google Analytics 4, Microsoft Clarity, Meta Pixel).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your RFQ and provide quotes
  • To send product updates and newsletters (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • To improve our website and marketing
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Sharing of Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share only with shipping carriers, payment processors, and analytics providers acting as data processors under contract.

5. International Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including China, the United States, and the European Economic Area. We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards for cross-border transfers.

6. Data Retention

RFQ inquiries: 24 months for legitimate business purposes. Newsletter subscriptions: until you unsubscribe. Analytics: 14 months (Google Analytics default).

7. Your Rights

Under GDPR, CCPA and similar laws, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, port, or object to the processing of your personal data. Email privacy@stagelumen.com to exercise these rights.

8. Cookies & Tracking

See our Cookie Policy for details on the cookies and tracking technologies we use.

9. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (TLS 1.3) and access controls. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

10. Children

Our Site is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email or a banner on the Site.
