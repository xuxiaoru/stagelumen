Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Who We Are

GuangZhou GeLiang Lighting Technology Co., Ltd. ("we", "us", "our") operates the website www.rigebalighting.com (the "Site"), with its registered office at No. 21, Lingdong Road, Xiuquan Street, Huadu District, Guangzhou, China. We are a manufacturer of professional stage lighting based in Guangzhou, China.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when submitting a quote request, downloading resources, subscribing to our newsletter, contacting sales, or chatting with our on-site assistant. This may include: name, company, country, email, phone number, project details, and the content of any message you send us.

We also collect limited technical data automatically — page viewed, referrer, country derived from your IP address and browser type — using our own first-party analytics. We do not load third-party advertising or analytics trackers such as Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity or Meta Pixel on this Site.

AI assistant conversations. Messages you type into the RiGeBa Lighting sales assistant, and the answers it generates, are stored so our sales team can review the thread, take it over when the assistant cannot finish it, and improve its knowledge base. Transcripts are visible only to authorised staff and are never used to train third-party models.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your RFQ and provide quotes
  • To answer questions through the AI assistant and hand the conversation to a human where needed
  • To send product updates and newsletters (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • To improve our website and marketing
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Sharing of Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share only with shipping carriers, payment processors, and analytics providers acting as data processors under contract.

5. International Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including China, the United States, and the European Economic Area. We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards for cross-border transfers.

6. Data Retention

RFQ inquiries: 24 months for legitimate business purposes. Newsletter subscriptions: until you unsubscribe. Analytics: 14 months. AI assistant conversations: 180 days, after which they are permanently deleted; when a conversation turns into a sales inquiry, the resulting lead record is kept for the RFQ period above. You can ask us to delete your conversation earlier at sales20@rigelighting.com.

7. Your Rights

Under GDPR, CCPA and similar laws, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, port, or object to the processing of your personal data. Email sales20@rigelighting.com to exercise these rights.

8. Cookies & Tracking

See our Cookie Policy for details on the cookies and tracking technologies we use.

9. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (TLS 1.3) and access controls. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

10. Children

Our Site is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email or a banner on the Site.
