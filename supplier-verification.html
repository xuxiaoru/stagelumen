Is RiGeBa Lighting a Real Stage Lighting Factory?

Before a first order, buyers need to know who they are contracting with, whether the factory exists, and which claims can be checked against a third party. This page gives the legal entity, the business licence, the onsite assessment and the verifiable external profile in one place, with links to the underlying documents.

The short answer. RiGeBa Lighting is the trading brand of Guangzhou Geliang Lighting Technology Co., Ltd., Unified Social Credit Code 914401145544427915, registered in Huadu District, Guangzhou since 18 May 2010. We run a 10,000 m² factory with 300 staff and publish 632 catalogue models, and we make our business licence, an SGS-conducted supplier assessment and four CE test certificates available for download.

Scope

How do I check a certificate against a specific model?

Certificates are issued for named tested models, not for a whole catalogue. We matched the 76 model codes printed across the four certificates to our 632 product IDs: 40 codes match a live SKU (6.3%). Do not read a certificate as covering a model that is absent from its model schedule.

Commercial terms

What should be stated in the quotation?

We do not publish fixed figures for minimum order quantity, production lead time, payment schedule or warranty period. All four are stated in the order confirmation for the specific order, alongside the model codes, quantities and destination market. Compliance documentation for the destination market is supplied with the quotation on request, and only for documents that actually exist for those models.

Questions buyers ask before a first order

Is RiGeBa Lighting a manufacturer or a trading company?

We are the factory: a 10,000 m² facility in Huadu District, Guangzhou with 300 staff, an in-house SMT line and aging test before packing, operating since 18 May 2010. The redacted business licence on this page shows the registered entity and address.

What is your Unified Social Credit Code?

914401145544427915. It is printed on the business licence and is the reliable way to disambiguate the company from the historic English spelling used on certificates issued before November 2026.

Can I verify the company on a third-party site?

Yes. The Alibaba.com Gold Plus / Verified Supplier storefront at rigelight.en.alibaba.com returns HTTP 200 and its company overview cites Guangzhou Geliang Lighting Technology Co., Ltd. The storefront assessment was conducted onsite by SGS under certificate 498917075_P+T, valid to 21 October 2027. We checked the profile on 17 November 2026.

Which documents can I download right now?

A redacted business licence, four CE certificates (two EMC/LVD pairs) and the Alibaba Gold Plus supplier assessment. Each is listed with its certificate number, issuing body, standards and covered models on the certificates page.

Do your certificates cover all 632 models?

No. 76 model codes appear across the four certificates and 40 of them match a live catalogue SKU, about 6.3% of the range. Each certificate applies to the tested sample only and does not imply an assessment of the whole production. Check the model schedule for the exact codes in your order.

What commercial terms will the quotation state?

MOQ, lead time, payment schedule and warranty coverage are stated in the order confirmation for that order. We do not publish fixed numbers on this site, and we only issue compliance documents that actually exist for the models being quoted.

Want to verify something we have not listed?
