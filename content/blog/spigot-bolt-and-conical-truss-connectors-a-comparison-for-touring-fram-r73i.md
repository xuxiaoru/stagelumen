Spigot, bolt, and conical truss connectors are essential components of touring frames, providing a secure and stable connection for lighting and equipment. The choice of connector depends on the specific needs of the frame, including size, weight, and frequency of setup and teardown.

## The short answer
For a touring frame, a spigot 90° square truss with four face connector, such as the WT-L1F30d90C-W30, can provide a quick and easy connection, with an EXW price of US$162.8 per unit.

## What are the advantages of spigot truss connectors?
Spigot truss connectors are designed for quick and easy connection, making them ideal for touring frames that require frequent setup and teardown. They are also lightweight and compact, making them easy to transport and store.

## How do bolt truss connectors compare to spigot connectors?
Bolt truss connectors provide a more secure and permanent connection than spigot connectors, making them ideal for large and heavy touring frames. However, they can be more time-consuming to set up and teardown, and may require additional tools and equipment.

## What are the key considerations when choosing a truss connector?
When choosing a truss connector, consider the size and weight of the frame, the type of lighting or equipment being used, and the frequency of setup and teardown. It's also important to consider the budget and the level of security required.

## How do conical truss connectors work?
Conical truss connectors use a conical shape to provide a secure and stable connection. They are easy to use and require minimal maintenance, making them ideal for large and heavy touring frames.

## What are the benefits of using conical truss connectors?
Conical truss connectors provide a high level of security and stability, making them ideal for large and heavy touring frames. They are also easy to use and require minimal maintenance, reducing the risk of damage or wear.

## Can I use spigot truss connectors for large touring frames?
Yes, spigot truss connectors can be used for large touring frames, but it's essential to ensure that they are designed for large and heavy loads. It's also important to follow proper setup and teardown procedures to ensure safety.

## How do I ensure the security of my truss connectors?
To ensure the security of your truss connectors, always follow proper setup and teardown procedures, and make sure that all connections are secure. It's also important to regularly inspect the connectors for damage or wear.

## What is the difference between a 90° and 45° truss connector?
A 90° truss connector, such as the WT-L1F30d90C-W30, provides a secure and stable connection for perpendicular frames, while a 45° truss connector, such as the WT-L1F30b45C-W30, provides a secure and stable connection for diagonal frames.

## How do I choose the right angle for my truss connector?
To choose the right angle for your truss connector, consider the design of the touring frame and the type of lighting or equipment being used. It's also important to consider the budget and the level of security required.
