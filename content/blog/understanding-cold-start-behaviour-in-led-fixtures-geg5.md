Cold-start behaviour is a critical consideration for technicians working with LED fixtures, particularly in outdoor or cold-weather applications. As temperatures drop, LED fixtures can experience a reduction in performance, leading to decreased brightness and slower response times. This can have a significant impact on the overall lighting design and require adjustments to be made to compensate for the reduced output.

## What is cold-start behaviour?
Cold-start behaviour refers to the reduced performance of LED fixtures in low temperatures, typically below 0°C. This can result in decreased brightness, slower response times, and reduced overall efficiency.
