What is the difference between haze and fog?

Haze is a thin, uniform mist that hangs in the air, while fog is a thicker, more dense mist that lies closer to the ground. The choice between haze and fog depends on the desired effect and the size of the venue. For example, haze is often used in theatrical productions to create a sense of depth and atmosphere, while fog is often used in concerts and festivals to create a more immersive experience.

How do I choose the right atmospheric machine for my venue?
