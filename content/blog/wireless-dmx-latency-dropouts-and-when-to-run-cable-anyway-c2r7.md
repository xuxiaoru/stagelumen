## What is wireless DMX and how does it work?
Wireless DMX is a technology that allows for the transmission of DMX signals over a wireless connection, eliminating the need for cables. It works by using a transmitter to send the DMX signal to a receiver, which then converts the signal back into a wired DMX connection. This technology is useful for applications where cables are not feasible or would be a hindrance.

## What are the advantages of wireless DMX?
The main advantage of wireless DMX is its convenience and flexibility. It allows for easier setup and teardown, and eliminates the need for cables, which can be bulky and heavy. Wireless DMX also provides more freedom to move lights and other devices around the venue without being restricted by cables.

## What are the limitations of wireless DMX?
One of the main limitations of wireless DMX is its susceptibility to interference and dropouts. This can be caused by other devices in the vicinity, such as wireless microphones or cell phones, or by physical barriers, such as walls or pillars. Additionally, wireless DMX systems can be affected by latency, which can cause delays in the transmission of the DMX signal.

To troubleshoot your wireless DMX system, check the transmitter and receiver for any signs of damage or interference.
