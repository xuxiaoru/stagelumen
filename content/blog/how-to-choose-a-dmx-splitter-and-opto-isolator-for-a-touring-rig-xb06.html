How to Choose a DMX Splitter and Opto-Isolator for a Touring Rig

Choose a DMX splitter with IP65 rating

October 4, 2026 · RiGeBa Lighting Team · How-To

DMX splitters and opto-isolators are essential components in a touring rig, as they allow you to split and isolate the DMX signal to multiple devices. Choosing the right DMX splitter and opto-isolator can be a daunting task, especially with the numerous options available in the market.

The short answer

To choose a DMX splitter, consider the number of outputs you need, with options like the RG-CA8802 DMX Splitter starting at $51 for 8 outputs.

What is a DMX splitter and how does it work?

A DMX splitter is a device that takes a DMX signal and splits it into multiple outputs, allowing you to control multiple devices from a single DMX controller. The splitter works by amplifying the DMX signal and then splitting it into multiple outputs, each of which can be connected to a separate device.

What are the key considerations when choosing a DMX splitter?

When choosing a DMX splitter, there are several key considerations to keep in mind. The first is the number of outputs you need, as this will determine the size and complexity of the splitter. You should also consider the distance between devices, as this can affect the strength and quality of the DMX signal. Additionally, you should consider the level of electrical noise in your system, as this can affect the performance of the splitter.

How do I determine the right size and configuration for my DMX splitter?

To determine the right size and configuration for your DMX splitter, you should consider the specific needs of your touring rig. You should start by determining the number of outputs you need, and then choose a splitter that can accommodate that number. You should also consider the distance between devices and the level of electrical noise in your system, and choose a splitter that is designed to handle those conditions.

What is the difference between a DMX splitter and an opto-isolator?

A DMX splitter is used to split a DMX signal into multiple outputs, while an opto-isolator is used to isolate the DMX signal from electrical noise. The RG-CA8802 DMX Splitter and the RG-CA8402C Waterproof DMX Splitter are examples of DMX splitters that can be used in a touring rig. The RG-CA8402C Waterproof DMX Splitter has an IP65 rating, making it suitable for outdoor use.

How do I choose the right opto-isolator for my DMX system?

To choose the right opto-isolator for your DMX system, you should consider the level of electrical noise in your system and the type of devices you are using. You should choose an opto-isolator that is designed to handle the specific conditions of your system, and that can provide the necessary isolation to prevent electrical noise from interfering with the DMX signal.

What are the benefits of using a DMX splitter with an opto-isolator?

Using a DMX splitter with an opto-isolator can provide several benefits, including improved signal quality and reduced electrical noise. The opto-isolator can help to prevent electrical noise from interfering with the DMX signal, which can cause errors or damage to equipment. The DMX splitter can then take the isolated signal and split it into multiple outputs, allowing you to control multiple devices from a single DMX controller.

How do I troubleshoot a DMX splitter and opto-isolator?

To troubleshoot a DMX splitter and opto-isolator, you should start by checking the connections to make sure they are secure. You should then check the DMX signal to make sure it is strong and free of noise. If you are using an opto-isolator, you should check to make sure it is properly configured and that it is providing the necessary isolation. You can also try swapping out the splitter or opto-isolator with a different one to see if the problem is with the device itself.
