Corporate conferences require careful lighting design to ensure that the presenter and screen are well-lit, while minimizing spill and glare. The right lighting can make a significant difference in the overall quality of the event.

## The short answer
Use a 300W LED Panel light for front light, with a beam angle of 2º to 8 meters, and consider a separate screen wash fixture to reduce glare.

## What are the key considerations when choosing a front light fixture?
When choosing a front light fixture, consider factors such as brightness, color temperature, and control options. The 300W LED Panel light is a good option, with a color temperature of 3200K/5600K and a control option of master control/DMX.

## How do I determine the right size and configuration for my front light?
To determine the right size and configuration for your front light, consider the size of the room and the distance between the lights and the presenter. A larger room may require more fixtures or fixtures with a higher brightness.

## What is the purpose of screen wash in a corporate conference?
Screen wash is used to illuminate the screen and reduce glare, making it easier for the audience to see the presentation. It can be achieved using a separate lighting fixture or by adjusting the front light to also illuminate the screen.

## How can I achieve screen wash using a separate lighting fixture?
To achieve screen wash using a separate lighting fixture, consider using a fixture with a wide beam angle and a high color rendering index. The [RG-M300BH-KYH16M](/products/moving/rg-m300bh-kyh16m) is a good option, with a beam angle of 16º and a color rendering index of Ra≥90.

## What are the benefits of using a DMX controller for my lighting system?
Using a DMX controller, such as the [RG-CA8802PRO-A](/products/controller/rg-ca8802pro-a), can provide more precise control over your lighting system, allowing you to adjust the brightness and color of each fixture individually.

## How do I keep spill off the presenter?
To keep spill off the presenter, use a combination of careful lighting placement, beam angle control, and barn doors or shutters to direct the light where it is needed. Consider using a fixture with a high color rendering index, such as the 300W LED Panel light, to create a more natural and flattering light.

## What is the typical power consumption of a corporate conference lighting system?
The typical power consumption of a corporate conference lighting system depends on the number and type of fixtures used. For example, the 300W LED Panel light has a power consumption of 450W.

## How can I reduce the power consumption of my lighting system?
To reduce the power consumption of your lighting system, consider using energy-efficient fixtures, such as LED lights, and adjusting the brightness and color of each fixture to minimize waste.
