Movement macros on moving heads are a crucial aspect of lighting design, allowing technicians to create intentional and repetitive movements that enhance the visual effect of a performance or event. For technicians working with moving heads, understanding how to create and implement movement macros is essential. 

## The short answer
To create intentional movement macros on moving heads, use a moving head with DMX control, such as the 6W Moving head full color laser, and program it using a lighting control console or software.

## What is a movement macro?
A movement macro is a pre-programmed sequence of movements that can be repeated and varied to create a desired visual effect. Movement macros can be used to create complex patterns and designs, such as circles, ballyhoo, and figure-8 cues.

## How do I choose the right moving head for my lighting design?

## What are the key considerations when programming movement macros?
When programming movement macros, consider the speed and accuracy of the movement, the size and shape of the pattern, and the overall visual effect. It's also essential to ensure that the moving head is properly calibrated and maintained to ensure smooth and precise movement.

## How do I program a circle movement macro?
