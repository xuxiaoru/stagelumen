## What is the relationship between beam angle and output?
The beam angle and output of a light are related in that a narrower beam angle can result in a brighter reading, even if the output is lower. This is because the narrower angle concentrates the light, resulting in a higher intensity reading. For example, a 230W beam with a narrow angle can appear brighter than a 400W wash with a wider angle.

Beam lights and wash lights differ in their beam angle and intended application. Beam lights are designed for focused lighting and typically have a narrower beam angle, while wash lights are designed for more even coverage and have a wider angle.
