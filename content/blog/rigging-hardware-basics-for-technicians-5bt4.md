## What is the purpose of a clamp in rigging?
A clamp is used to secure a load to a fixed point, such as a truss or beam, and is an essential component of rigging hardware. ## What is the difference between a coupler and a safety bond?
A coupler is used to connect two or more loads together, while a safety bond is used to secure a load to a fixed point, preventing it from falling or shifting during a show. Both are essential components of rigging hardware and should be inspected regularly for signs of wear or damage.

## How do I inspect my rigging hardware before a show?
Before every show, inspect your rigging hardware, including clamps, couplers, and safety bonds, to ensure that all components are properly secured and functioning as intended. Check for signs of wear, damage, or corrosion, and ensure that all components are properly rated for the weight and size of the load.

## What are the key considerations when using a safety bond?
When using a safety bond, consider the type of load being lifted, the weight and size of the load, and the material of the safety bond. Ensure that the safety bond is properly rated for the weight and size of the load, and that it is installed correctly.

## How do I determine the right size and configuration for my safety bond?
To determine the right size and configuration for your safety bond, consider the specific requirements of your show or event, including the type of load being lifted, the weight and size of the load, and the available rigging points. Consult with a qualified rigger or technician if you are unsure.
