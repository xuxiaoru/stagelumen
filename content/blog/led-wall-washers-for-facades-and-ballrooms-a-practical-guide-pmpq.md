## How do I determine the throw distance for my LED wall washer?
To determine the throw distance for your LED wall washer, you need to consider the size of the area you want to illuminate and the desired level of brightness. A general rule of thumb is to use a throw distance of 1-2 times the height of the facade. For example, if the facade is 10 meters high, you would use a throw distance of 10-20 meters.
