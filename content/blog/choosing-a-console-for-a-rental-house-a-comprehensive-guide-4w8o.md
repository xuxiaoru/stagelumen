## How do I determine the right universe count for my console?
To determine the right universe count for your console, consider the number of DMX devices you need to control and the number of channels each device requires. A general rule of thumb is to allocate 1-2 universes per 100 devices. For example, if you have 200 devices, you may need 2-4 universes. The RG-CS0302 DMX512 mixer offers expandable output ports, which can enhance the equipment load capacity and provide more universes as needed.

## What is the difference between a DMX512 mixer and a lighting controller?
A DMX512 mixer, such as the RG-CS0302, is designed to mix and control multiple DMX signals, while a lighting controller, such as the RG-CTD40S6F-K, is designed to control and dim lighting fixtures. The RG-CS0302 offers expandable output ports and can enhance the equipment load capacity, making it a more versatile option for larger events or more complex lighting setups.
