Profile spot and fresnel lenses are two popular types of lighting fixtures used in theatres. Both have their own strengths and weaknesses, and choosing the right one for a small theatre can be a daunting task. The decision ultimately depends on the specific needs of the theatre, including the size of the stage, the type of performances, and the desired lighting effects.

## The short answer
For a small theatre, the RG-PS200A20T50-W profile spot is a cost-effective option at $95, while the RG-PS200A20T50-K fresnel lens offers more versatility at $168.

## What is a profile spot and how does it work?
A profile spot is a type of lighting fixture that uses a fixed beam angle to produce a focused beam of light. It is typically used to highlight specific areas of the stage or to create dramatic lighting effects. Profile spots are often used in conjunction with other lighting fixtures to create a layered lighting design.

## What is a fresnel lens and how does it work?
A fresnel lens is a type of lighting fixture that uses a zoomable beam angle to produce a flexible beam of light. It is typically used to provide a wide range of lighting effects, from narrow spotlights to wide floodlights. Fresnel lenses are often used in theatres with dynamic stages, where the lighting requirements change frequently.

## What are the advantages of using a profile spot?
Profile spots are generally less expensive than fresnel lenses, with prices starting at $95 for the RG-PS200A20T50-W. They are also often lighter and more compact, making them easier to install and transport. Additionally, profile spots are often more energy-efficient, with lower power consumption and heat dissipation.

## What are the disadvantages of using a profile spot?
One of the main disadvantages of using a profile spot is its fixed beam angle, which can limit its versatility. Profile spots are also often less bright than fresnel lenses, which can make them less effective for larger stages or more dramatic lighting effects.

## What are the advantages of using a fresnel lens?
Fresnel lenses are highly versatile, with zoomable beam angles that can produce a wide range of lighting effects. They are also often brighter than profile spots, making them more effective for larger stages or more dramatic lighting effects. Additionally, fresnel lenses are often more durable, with longer lifetimes and less maintenance required.

## What are the disadvantages of using a fresnel lens?
One of the main disadvantages of using a fresnel lens is its higher cost, with prices starting at $168 for the RG-PS200A20T50-K. Fresnel lenses are also often heavier and more complex, making them more difficult to install and transport. Additionally, fresnel lenses often require more power and produce more heat, which can increase energy costs and require additional cooling systems.

## How do I choose between a profile spot and a fresnel lens?
To choose between a profile spot and a fresnel lens, consider the specific needs of your theatre, including the size of the stage, the type of performances, and the desired lighting effects. You should also think about the budget, the desired level of versatility, and the required level of brightness.
