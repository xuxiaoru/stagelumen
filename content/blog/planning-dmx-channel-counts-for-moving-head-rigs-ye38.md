<p>## Understanding DMX Channel Requirements</p>

When planning a moving head rig, it's essential to consider the number of DMX channels required for each fixture. This ensures that the lighting console can control all the necessary parameters, such as dimming, color, and gobo movement.

## Calculating DMX Channels for Dimming

Most moving heads use 16-bit dimming, which requires 1 DMX channel per fixture. However, some fixtures may have additional dimming channels for specific functions, such as iris or frost. For example, the SL-B150 uses 1 DMX channel for dimming, while the SL-B400 uses 1 DMX channel for dimming and 1 additional channel for iris control.

## Considering Color and Gobo Movement

Color wheels and gobos typically require 1 DMX channel each. However, some fixtures may have multiple color wheels or gobos that require additional channels. For example, the SL-B600 has a color wheel that requires 1 DMX channel, while the SL-S200 has a rotating gobo that requires 1 DMX channel.

## Accounting for Pan and Tilt

Pan and tilt movements typically require 1 DMX channel each. However, some fixtures may have additional pan and tilt channels for specific functions, such as smooth or stepped movement. For example, the SL-B280 has a 540° pan movement that requires 1 DMX channel, while the SL-B400 has a 540° pan movement that requires 1 DMX channel and 1 additional channel for smooth movement.

## Planning for Multiple Fixtures

When planning a moving head rig with multiple fixtures, it's essential to consider the total number of DMX channels required. This can be calculated by multiplying the number of fixtures by the number of DMX channels required per fixture. For example, a rig with 10 SL-B150 fixtures would require 10 x 16 = 160 DMX channels.

## Selecting the Right Console

When selecting a lighting console, it's essential to consider the number of DMX channels it can support. Most consoles have a limited number of DMX channels available, so it's crucial to choose a console that can handle the total number of channels required for the rig.

## Conclusion

Planning DMX channel counts for a moving head rig requires careful consideration of the number of channels required for dimming, color, gobo movement, pan, and tilt. By calculating the total number of channels required and selecting the right console, technicians can ensure that their rig runs smoothly and efficiently.

## Next Steps

* Program the lighting console to control the moving head rig.
