## What is panel pitch and why is it important?
Panel pitch refers to the distance between each LED pixel on the dance floor. A smaller panel pitch, such as 2.976mm, will provide a higher resolution and more detailed image, while a larger panel pitch, such as 3.91mm, will provide a lower resolution and less detailed image. The panel pitch is important because it affects the overall visual quality of the dance floor.

## How do I determine the right panel pitch for my LED dance floor?
To determine the right panel pitch for your LED dance floor, you need to consider the viewing distance and the desired level of detail. A smaller panel pitch is suitable for closer viewing distances and more detailed images, while a larger panel pitch is suitable for farther viewing distances and less detailed images.

## What is weight loading and how do I determine the right weight loading for my LED dance floor?
Weight loading refers to the maximum weight that the dance floor can support. To determine the right weight loading for your LED dance floor, you need to consider the expected foot traffic and the weight of the dancers.
