## What is the difference between haze and fog?
Haze is a thin, uniform mist that hangs in the air, while fog is a thicker, more dense mist that lies closer to the ground. The choice between haze and fog depends on the desired effect and the size of the venue. For example, a small room may require a haze machine, while a large room may require a fog machine.
