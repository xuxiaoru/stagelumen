## What are the different shipping terms?
There are several shipping terms, including EXW, FOB, CIF, and DDP. Each term defines the point at which the risk and cost of shipping are transferred from the seller to the buyer.

## How does EXW shipping term work?
EXW (Ex Works) means that the seller is responsible for making the goods available at their premises. The buyer is responsible for loading the goods onto the ship and paying for all costs associated with shipping.

## What are the benefits of using FOB shipping term?
FOB (Free on Board) means that the seller is responsible for loading the goods onto the ship. This can simplify the shipping process and reduce the risk of damage or loss during loading.

## How does CIF shipping term affect the cost of goods?
CIF (Cost, Insurance, and Freight) includes the cost of the goods, insurance, and freight. This means that the seller is responsible for the cost of shipping and insurance, which can increase the overall cost of the goods.

## What are the benefits of using DDP shipping term?
DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) means that the seller is responsible for all costs, including duties and taxes, up to the point of delivery. This can simplify the import process and reduce the risk of unexpected costs for the buyer.

## What are the key differences between EXW and FOB shipping terms?

When shipping goods, it's essential to understand the differences between EXW (Ex Works) and FOB (Free on Board) shipping terms. EXW means that the seller is responsible for making the goods available at their premises, and the buyer is responsible for loading and transporting the goods. In contrast, FOB means that the seller is responsible for loading the goods onto a carrier, and the buyer is responsible for the transportation costs. The key difference between the two is that EXW requires the buyer to take on more responsibility for the goods, while FOB shifts some of that responsibility to the seller. For example, if a buyer orders a lighting system from RiGeBa Lighting and chooses EXW, they will be responsible for loading and transporting the system to their premises. However, if they choose FOB, RiGeBa Lighting will be responsible for loading the system onto a carrier, and the buyer will only be responsible for the transportation costs. Understanding these differences is crucial for businesses to make informed decisions about their shipping needs.
