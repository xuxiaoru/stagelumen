Profile spot and fresnel lenses are two common types of lenses used in theatre lighting. Each has its own unique characteristics and advantages, and choosing the right one for a small theatre can be a difficult decision.

## What is a profile spot lens?
A profile spot lens is a type of lens that uses an elliptical reflector and a lens to create a sharp, defined beam of light. This type of lens is often used in theatre lighting to create a focused beam of light that can be used to highlight specific areas of the stage.

## What is a fresnel lens?
A fresnel lens is a type of lens that uses a series of concentric rings to focus the light. This type of lens is often used in theatre lighting to create a softer, more diffuse light that can be used to illuminate larger areas of the stage.

## What are the advantages of a profile spot lens?
The advantages of a profile spot lens include its ability to create a sharp, defined beam of light and its high level of precision and control. This makes it ideal for applications where a specific area of the stage needs to be highlighted.

## What are the disadvantages of a profile spot lens?
The disadvantages of a profile spot lens include its limited beam angle and its potential for hot spots. This can make it less suitable for applications where a wider beam angle is required.

## What are the advantages of a fresnel lens?
The advantages of a fresnel lens include its ability to create a softer, more diffuse light and its wider beam angle. This makes it ideal for applications where a larger area of the stage needs to be illuminated.

## What are the disadvantages of a fresnel lens?
The disadvantages of a fresnel lens include its lower level of precision and control and its potential for a less defined beam. This can make it less suitable for applications where a sharp, defined beam is required.

## How do I choose between a profile spot and a fresnel lens?
To choose between a profile spot and a fresnel lens, you should consider the specific needs of your theatre. If you need a sharp, defined beam of light, a profile spot may be the better choice. If you need a softer, more diffuse light, a fresnel lens may be more suitable.
