## Introduction to Gobo and Prism Effects
Gobo and prism effects in moving heads are powerful tools for creating engaging and dynamic lighting designs. These effects can add texture, depth, and visual interest to a performance or event. In this tutorial, we will explore the practical creative uses of gobo and prism effects in moving heads.

## Understanding Gobo Effects
Gobo effects are achieved by projecting a pattern or image onto a surface using a gobo wheel. The gobo wheel is typically made up of multiple gobos, each with a different pattern or design. For example, the RG-ML150BD-KEE1H16 LED Zoom Moving Head 3in1 has a gobo wheel with 5 gobos + 2 open, allowing for a variety of different effects.

## Understanding Prism Effects
Prism effects, on the other hand, are achieved by refracting light through a prism, creating a range of colors and patterns. The RG-ML150BR-KNNW1H17 LED 150W Beam Moving Head has a rotating 8+16 prism, which can create a range of dynamic effects.
