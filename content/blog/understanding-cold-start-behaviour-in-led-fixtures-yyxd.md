## What is cold-start behaviour?
Cold-start behaviour refers to the reduced performance of LED fixtures in low-temperature environments. This is due to the decreased efficiency of the LEDs and other components at lower temperatures. As the temperature drops, the LEDs' ability to produce light decreases, resulting in reduced light output and decreased colour accuracy.

## How does cold-start behaviour affect LED fixtures?
Cold-start behaviour can cause LED fixtures to derate, resulting in reduced light output, decreased colour accuracy, and increased risk of component failure. The extent of derating depends on the specific fixture, its temperature rating, and the ambient temperature. For example, the RG-P58TC10-Ke18A, priced at USD 149, may experience significant derating in extremely cold temperatures.
