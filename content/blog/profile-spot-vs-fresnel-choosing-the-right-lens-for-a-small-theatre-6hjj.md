## What is a profile spot and how does it work?
A profile spot is a type of stage light that uses a fixed lens to focus the light into a narrow beam. This makes it ideal for highlighting specific areas of the stage or for creating sharp, defined shadows. Profile spots are often used in theatres with a fixed stage size, where the beam angle can be precisely controlled.

## What is a fresnel lens and how does it work?
A fresnel lens is a type of stage light that uses a zoomable lens to focus the light into a variable beam angle. This makes it ideal for theatres with a variable stage size, where the beam angle needs to be adjusted to accommodate different performances. Fresnel lenses are often used in theatres that host a variety of performances, from plays to musicals to dance recitals.

## What are the advantages of a profile spot?
The advantages of a profile spot include its low cost, reliability, and ease of use. Profile spots are often less expensive than fresnel lenses, making them a good choice for small theatres on a tight budget. They are also relatively simple to operate, with a fixed beam angle that requires minimal adjustment.

## What are the disadvantages of a profile spot?
The disadvantages of a profile spot include its limited versatility and fixed beam angle. Profile spots are not ideal for theatres with a variable stage size, as the fixed beam angle can make it difficult to adjust the light to accommodate different performances.

## What are the advantages of a fresnel lens?
The advantages of a fresnel lens include its versatility, zoomable beam angle, and ability to adapt to different stage sizes. Fresnel lenses are ideal for theatres that host a variety of performances, as they can be easily adjusted to accommodate different beam angles and stage sizes.

## What are the disadvantages of a fresnel lens?
The disadvantages of a fresnel lens include its higher cost, complexity, and weight. Fresnel lenses are often more expensive than profile spots, making them a significant investment for small theatres. They are also more complex to operate, with a zoomable beam angle that requires more adjustment and maintenance.

## How do I choose between a profile spot and a fresnel lens for my small theatre?
To choose between a profile spot and a fresnel lens for your small theatre, consider the size of your theatre, the type of performances you will be hosting, and your budget. If you need a versatile light with a zoomable beam angle, a fresnel lens may be the better choice. However, if you are on a tight budget and need a reliable, fixed-beam light, a profile spot may be the way to go.
