Pixel tube spacing and pitch are critical considerations when designing a backdrop for a stage or event. For a 6m backdrop, the number of pixel tubes needed will depend on the desired resolution, brightness, and color temperature. 

## The short answer
A 6m backdrop requires at least 10-20 pixel tubes, depending on the spacing and pitch, with a minimum spacing of 10mm to 50mm.

## What is pixel tube spacing and pitch?
Pixel tube spacing refers to the distance between individual LEDs on a tube, while pitch refers to the distance between the centers of adjacent tubes. Both spacing and pitch are important considerations when designing a pixel tube backdrop, as they affect the overall resolution and brightness of the display.

## How do I calculate the number of pixel tubes needed for a 6m backdrop?
To calculate the number of pixel tubes needed, you need to consider the spacing and pitch of the tubes, as well as the resolution and brightness required for your application. A general rule of thumb is to use 10-20 pixel tubes per meter of backdrop, depending on the desired resolution. For example, if you want a high-resolution display with a spacing of 10mm, you may need to use 20 pixel tubes per meter.

## What is the difference between DMX and Art-Net control modes?
DMX and Art-Net are both control modes used for pixel tubes, but they have different specifications and applications. DMX is a more traditional control mode, while Art-Net is a newer protocol that offers more flexibility and scalability. For example, the RG-W39IB1-Kf48A supports both DMX and Art-Net control modes, while the DMX Control Pixel LED Lifting Tube only supports DMX.

## How do I ensure even lighting with pixel tubes?
To ensure even lighting with pixel tubes, it's essential to carefully plan and calculate the spacing and pitch of the tubes, as well as the power supply and control system. You may also need to adjust the brightness and color temperature of the tubes to achieve the desired effect. For example, you can use a power supply with a high current rating to ensure that the tubes are evenly lit.
