## Introduction
When it comes to specifying a control protocol for a touring rig, the choice can be overwhelming. DMX, Art-Net, and sACN are three popular options, each with its own strengths and weaknesses. In this article, we will delve into the details of each protocol and provide a head-to-head comparison to help you make an informed decision.

## DMX Protocol
DMX is a widely used protocol for controlling lighting fixtures. It is a linear protocol that uses a single cable to transmit data between devices. DMX is commonly used in touring rigs and is supported by most lighting consoles.

## Art-Net Protocol
Art-Net is a network protocol that allows for the transmission of data between devices over a network. It is commonly used in live events and installations. Art-Net is known for its reliability and flexibility, but it requires a network infrastructure to function.

## sACN Protocol
sACN is a protocol that is similar to Art-Net, but it is more widely supported and has a lower latency. sACN is commonly used in touring rigs and is known for its reliability and flexibility.

## Comparison
| Protocol | DMX | Art-Net | sACN | 
| --- | --- | --- | --- | 
| Reliability | High | High | High | 
| Flexibility | Low | High | High | 
| Latency | High | Medium | Low | 
| Network Infrastructure | No | Yes | No | 
| Cost | Low | Medium | High | 
| Complexity | Low | Medium | High | 
## Conclusion
In conclusion, the choice of control protocol depends on the specific needs of your touring rig. DMX is a good choice for touring rigs that require a simple and reliable solution, while Art-Net and sACN offer more flexibility and reliability for live events and installations.

## Next Steps
Before making a final decision, consider the following factors:
* The type of lighting fixtures being used
