OEM Stage Lighting: A Buyer's Guide

Choose the right fixture type with our selection criteria

September 11, 2026 · StageLumen Team · How-To

Selecting the Right Fixture Type

When it comes to OEM stage lighting, selecting the right fixture type can be a daunting task. With numerous options available, it's essential to consider several factors to ensure you choose the best one for your needs. Here are some key selection criteria to consider:

Wattage

The wattage of a fixture determines its brightness and energy consumption. A higher wattage fixture will provide more light, but it will also consume more energy and potentially increase your electricity bill.

  • For small venues and churches, a lower wattage fixture (e.g., 100W) may be sufficient.
  • For larger venues and touring productions, a higher wattage fixture (e.g., 500W) may be necessary.

Beam Angle

The beam angle of a fixture determines the spread of its light. A narrower beam angle (e.g., 10°) provides a more focused beam, while a wider beam angle (e.g., 60°) provides a broader coverage area.

  • For uplighting and spotlights, a narrower beam angle (e.g., 10°) is often preferred.
  • For general lighting and washes, a wider beam angle (e.g., 60°) is often preferred.

IP Rating

The IP rating of a fixture determines its level of protection against dust and water. A higher IP rating (e.g., IP65) provides better protection against dust and water.

  • For outdoor or wet locations, a higher IP rating (e.g., IP65) is essential.
  • For indoor locations, a lower IP rating (e.g., IP20) may be sufficient.

Control Protocol

The control protocol of a fixture determines how it can be controlled. Common control protocols include DMX512, Art-Net, and sACN.

  • For DMX512 control, look for fixtures with a DMX512 output.
  • For Art-Net and sACN control, look for fixtures with an Art-Net or sACN output.

Evaluating Fixtures

Once you have selected a fixture type, it's essential to evaluate its features and specifications. Here are some key factors to consider:

  • Universes: The number of universes a fixture can control determines its flexibility and scalability.
  • Output: The type and number of outputs a fixture has determines its compatibility with other fixtures and control systems.
  • Playbacks: The number of playbacks a fixture can perform determines its ability to store and recall complex lighting scenes.

OEM Stage Lighting from Brief to First Container

When working with an OEM, it's essential to consider the following steps:

  1. Define your requirements: Determine your lighting needs and select the right fixture type.
  2. Evaluate fixtures: Consider the features and specifications of potential fixtures.
  3. Create a brief: Develop a detailed brief outlining your requirements and expectations.
  4. Confirm feasibility: Work with the OEM to confirm the feasibility of your project and the tooling costs involved.
  5. Place your order: Once you have confirmed the details, place your order and receive your first container of OEM stage lighting.

Conclusion

OEM stage lighting can be a complex and time-consuming process, but with the right guidance and planning, you can ensure a successful outcome. By considering the key selection criteria and evaluating fixtures carefully, you can choose the right fixture type for your needs and receive high-quality OEM stage lighting from a trusted manufacturer.
