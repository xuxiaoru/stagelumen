## What are the key components of rigging hardware?
The key components of rigging hardware include clamps, couplers, and safety bonds. Clamps are used to attach rigging to the structure, while couplers are used to connect multiple pieces of rigging together. Safety bonds, on the other hand, are used to secure rigging hardware to the structure, preventing it from falling or shifting during use.

## How do I choose the right clamp for my rigging system?
Choosing the right clamp for your rigging system depends on several factors, including the type of structure, the weight of the equipment, and the desired level of security. For example, a clamp with a higher weight capacity may be required for heavier equipment, while a clamp with a lower profile may be preferred for smaller structures.

## What is the purpose of a coupler in rigging?
A coupler is used to connect multiple pieces of rigging together, allowing for a more complex and flexible rigging system. Couplers can be used to connect clamps, safety bonds, and other rigging hardware, and are available in a variety of sizes and types to suit different applications.

## How do I inspect my rigging hardware before a show?
Inspecting your rigging hardware before a show is crucial for ensuring a safe and successful event. This includes checking for signs of wear, damage, or corrosion, as well as verifying that all components are properly secured and functioning as intended. A sample inspection checklist might include:
| Component | Inspection Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| Clamps | Check for signs of wear or damage, verify proper securement |
| Couplers | Check for signs of wear or damage, verify proper connection |
| Safety Bonds | Check for signs of wear or damage, verify proper securement |

## What are the benefits of using a safety bond in rigging?
Using a safety bond in rigging provides an additional level of security and protection against equipment failure or accidental release. Safety bonds can help prevent damage to equipment and injury to personnel, and are an essential component of any rigging system.
