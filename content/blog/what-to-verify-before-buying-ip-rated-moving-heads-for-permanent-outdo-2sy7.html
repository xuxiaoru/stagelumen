What to Verify Before Buying IP-Rated Moving Heads for Permanent Outdoor Installs

Verify the IP rating, wattage, and control protocol before buying IP-rated moving heads for permanent outdoor installs.

September 23, 2026 · RiGeBa Lighting Team · How-To

Understanding IP Ratings

When selecting moving heads for permanent outdoor installs, it's essential to verify the IP rating. The IP rating, or Ingress Protection rating, indicates the level of protection against solid particles and water. For outdoor applications, a minimum IP65 rating is recommended. This rating ensures that the fixture can withstand dust and water jets from any direction.

Wattage and Beam Angle

The wattage and beam angle of the moving head are also crucial factors to consider. A higher wattage typically results in a brighter beam, but it may also increase the heat generated by the fixture. A wider beam angle can cover more area, but it may also reduce the intensity of the beam. For example, a 350W moving head with a 20° beam angle may be suitable for a small outdoor stage, while a 420W moving head with a 40° beam angle may be more suitable for a larger area.

Control Protocol

The control protocol of the moving head is also essential to verify. Popular control protocols include DMX, Art-Net, and sACN. Ensure that the moving head is compatible with your existing control system to avoid any potential issues.

Verifying Specifications

Before making a purchase, verify the specifications of the moving head, including the IP rating, wattage, beam angle, and control protocol. Check the manufacturer's website or contact their customer support to confirm the specifications.

Volume Discounts and Lead Times

If you plan to purchase multiple moving heads, consider asking about volume discounts and lead times. This can help you save money and ensure timely delivery of your order.

Sample Evaluation

Requesting a sample of the moving head can also be beneficial. This allows you to evaluate the fixture's performance, durability, and compatibility with your existing equipment.

NDA and Confidentiality

If you're working on a project that requires confidentiality, consider signing a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) with the manufacturer. This ensures that your design and specifications remain confidential.

Conclusion

In conclusion, verifying the IP rating, wattage, beam angle, control protocol, and specifications of the moving head is crucial before making a purchase. Additionally, considering volume discounts, lead times, and sample evaluation can help you make an informed decision. By taking these factors into account, you can ensure that your outdoor lighting installation meets your needs and provides a high-quality performance.
