## What is wireless DMX and how does it work?
Wireless DMX is a technology that allows for the transmission of DMX signals wirelessly, eliminating the need for cables. It uses radio frequencies to transmit the signals, which are then received by a receiver and converted back into a DMX signal. Wireless DMX systems typically consist of a transmitter, a receiver, and an antenna.

## What are the benefits of using wireless DMX?
The benefits of using wireless DMX include increased flexibility, convenience, and reduced cable clutter. Wireless DMX systems are ideal for applications where cables are not feasible or would be a hindrance, such as in outdoor events or in venues with complex layouts.

## What are the limitations of wireless DMX?
The limitations of wireless DMX include latency, dropouts, and interference. Latency refers to the delay between the transmission and reception of the signal, while dropouts refer to the loss of signal. Interference can be caused by other devices or systems operating on the same frequency.
