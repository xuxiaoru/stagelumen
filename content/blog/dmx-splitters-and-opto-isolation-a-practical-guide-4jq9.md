## What is a DMX splitter?
A DMX splitter is a device that takes a single DMX signal and splits it into multiple signals, allowing you to control multiple devices from a single universe. DMX splitters are available in different configurations, including 1-to-2, 1-to-4, and 1-to-8 splits.

## How does opto-isolation work?
Opto-isolation is a technique used in DMX splitters to prevent electrical noise and interference from affecting the DMX signal. It works by using a light-emitting diode (LED) to transmit the signal across an optical barrier, rather than using a direct electrical connection. This ensures that the signal remains clean and reliable, even over long cable runs.

## What are the benefits of using DMX splitters with opto-isolation?
Using DMX splitters with opto-isolation provides several benefits, including improved signal reliability, reduced electrical noise, and increased flexibility in system design. With opto-isolation, you can use longer cable runs and connect more devices to a single universe, making it easier to design and install large lighting systems.
