## What is PWM frequency and why is it important?
PWM frequency refers to the rate at which the fixture's LEDs are turned on and off. A higher PWM frequency results in a smoother and more stable image, while a lower frequency can produce flicker or artifacts. For high-speed camera shooting, a minimum PWM frequency of 10KHz is recommended.

## What is the relationship between PWM frequency and refresh rate?
The refresh rate of a pixel fixture refers to the rate at which the fixture updates its image. A higher refresh rate results in a smoother and more stable image, while a lower refresh rate can produce flicker or artifacts. The PWM frequency and refresh rate are related, as a higher PWM frequency requires a higher refresh rate to produce a smooth image.

## How do I choose the right pixel fixture for my application?
To choose the right pixel fixture for your application, consider the camera's shutter speed, the fixture's PWM frequency, and the refresh rate. A higher PWM frequency and refresh rate will result in a smoother and more stable image. Also, consider the fixture's beam angle and IP rating to ensure it can withstand the shooting environment.

## What are the key considerations when choosing a pixel fixture for outdoor use?
When choosing a pixel fixture for outdoor use, consider the fixture's IP rating, which should be at least IP65 to withstand the elements.
