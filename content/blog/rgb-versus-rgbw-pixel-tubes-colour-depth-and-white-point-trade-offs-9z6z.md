For professional buyers such as rental houses, touring productions, theatres, clubs, churches, and event companies, understanding the trade-offs between these two types of tubes is crucial for making informed decisions.

## What are the key differences between RGB and RGBW pixel tubes?
RGB and RGBW pixel tubes differ in their colour gamut and white point. RGB tubes typically have a wider colour gamut and can produce more vibrant and saturated colours, while RGBW tubes have a more neutral white point and can produce a higher level of brightness.

## How do RGBW pixel tubes achieve a higher level of brightness?
RGBW pixel tubes achieve a higher level of brightness by adding a white LED to the traditional RGB colour mix. This additional white LED provides a higher colour temperature and a more neutral white point, resulting in a brighter and more even light output.

## What are the trade-offs between colour depth and white point?
The trade-off between colour depth and white point is a critical consideration when choosing between RGB and RGBW pixel tubes. RGB tubes typically have a higher colour depth and can produce more vibrant and saturated colours, but may have a less neutral white point. RGBW tubes, on the other hand, have a more neutral white point and can produce a higher level of brightness, but may have a lower colour depth.

## What are the implications of using RGBW pixel tubes for colour-critical applications?
Using RGBW pixel tubes for colour-critical applications can result in a less vibrant and less saturated colour palette. This is because the additional white LED in RGBW tubes can reduce the colour depth and accuracy of the displayed colours. However, RGBW tubes can still be used for colour-critical applications if the specific requirements of the application prioritize brightness and white point over colour depth.

## How do I choose the right pixel tube for my application?
To choose the right pixel tube for your application, consider the specific requirements of your project, including the size and shape of the space, the type of content being displayed, and the desired level of colour accuracy and vibrancy. Additionally, think about the trade-offs between colour depth, white point, and brightness, and weigh the pros and cons of each option.
