Voltage and plug standards are crucial considerations for touring LED fixtures. Professional buyers, such as rental houses, touring productions, and event companies, need to ensure that their fixtures are compatible with the voltage standards of the regions they will be touring in. 

## The short answer
The RG-PS200A110-W operates at AC90-240V, making it suitable for both 110V and 220V standards.

## What voltage standards do I need to consider for touring LED fixtures?
When touring with LED fixtures, you need to consider both 110V and 220V standards, depending on the region you are touring in. The United States, Canada, and Japan, for example, use 110V, while most of Europe, Australia, and Asia use 220V. You should research the voltage standards of the regions you will be touring in and choose fixtures that are compatible with those standards.

## What are the benefits of using LED fixtures for touring productions?
LED fixtures offer several benefits for touring productions, including energy efficiency, durability, and versatility. They are also often lighter and more compact than traditional lighting fixtures, making them easier to transport and set up.

## How do I ensure that my LED fixtures are properly maintained and serviced during a tour?
You should regularly check and maintain your fixtures to ensure that they are functioning properly. You should also have a plan in place for servicing and repairing fixtures in case of malfunction or damage.

## What are the common problems that can occur with LED fixtures during a tour?
Common problems that can occur with LED fixtures during a tour include malfunction or damage due to incorrect voltage standard or plug type, overheating, and physical damage during transport. You should be prepared to troubleshoot and repair these issues to ensure that your production runs smoothly.
