## What is a DMX splitter and how does it work?

A DMX splitter is a device that splits the DMX signal from the controller to multiple devices, allowing for more devices to be controlled from a single controller. The DMX signal is a digital signal that contains the control data for the devices, and the splitter simply duplicates this signal and sends it to multiple outputs.

## What are the key considerations when choosing a DMX splitter?

When choosing a DMX splitter, there are several key considerations to keep in mind. The first is the number of outputs required, which will depend on the number of devices that need to be controlled. The second is the voltage and current requirements of the splitter and the devices it will be controlling. The third is the environment in which the splitter will be operating, including the temperature, humidity, and exposure to water and dust.

## How do I determine the right size and configuration for my DMX splitter?

To determine the right size and configuration for your DMX splitter, you need to consider the number of devices you need to control and the distance between them.
