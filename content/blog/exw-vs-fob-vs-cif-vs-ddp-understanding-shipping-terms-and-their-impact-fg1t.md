Shipping terms can have a significant impact on your invoice and risk when importing goods from overseas. For professional buyers, understanding the differences between EXW, FOB, CIF, and DDP shipping terms is crucial to making informed decisions.

## What is EXW shipping term?
EXW (Ex Works) is a shipping term where the seller is responsible for making the goods available at their premises. The buyer is responsible for loading the goods onto the ship, paying for transportation, and assuming all risks.

## How does FOB shipping term work?
FOB (Free on Board) means the seller is responsible for loading the goods onto the ship. The buyer is responsible for paying for transportation and assuming all risks after the goods are loaded.

## What is CIF shipping term?
CIF (Cost, Insurance, and Freight) includes the cost of insurance and freight in the seller's invoice. This shipping term provides protection against loss or damage during transit but can increase the overall cost.

## How does DDP shipping term affect my invoice?
DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) means the seller is responsible for all costs, including duties and taxes. This shipping term can simplify the buying process and reduce the buyer's risk but may also increase the overall cost.

## What are the benefits of using FOB shipping term?
FOB shipping term can be beneficial for buyers who want to control the transportation process and assume the risks. It can also be more cost-effective than CIF or DDP shipping terms.

## How do I choose the right shipping term for my business?
Consider factors such as the distance and mode of transportation, the value and fragility of the goods, and your company's risk tolerance and budget. It's also a good idea to consult with a logistics expert or RiGeBa Lighting's customer service team.

## What is the difference between CIF and DDP shipping terms?
CIF and DDP shipping terms both include the cost of insurance and freight, but DDP also includes duties and taxes. CIF shipping term requires the buyer to pay duties and taxes, while DDP shipping term includes these costs in the seller's invoice.
