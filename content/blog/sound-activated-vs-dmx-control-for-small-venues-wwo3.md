## What is sound-activated control?
Sound-activated control uses audio signals to trigger lighting effects. This type of control is often used in simple applications where precise control is not required. However, it can be limited in its flexibility and may not be suitable for more complex lighting systems.

## What is DMX control?
DMX control uses a dedicated protocol to control lighting fixtures. This type of control offers more precise and flexible control than sound-activated control and is generally better suited for small venues. DMX control can be used with a wide range of lighting fixtures, including LED lights, moving heads, and strobes.

## What are the benefits of DMX control?
The benefits of DMX control include more precise control, greater flexibility, and easier integration with other lighting fixtures. DMX control also allows for more complex lighting effects, such as chasing and fading, which can add to the overall ambiance of the venue.
