Cold-start behaviour is a critical consideration for technicians and lighting designers working with LED fixtures in cold weather conditions. When LED fixtures are first turned on in cold temperatures, they can experience a decrease in performance, known as derating. This can impact the overall lighting design and require adjustments to be made.

## The short answer
The 300W LED Panel light can experience derating in winter, with a power consumption of 450W and a voltage range of AC110-240V, 50-60HZ.

## What is cold-start behaviour?
Cold-start behaviour refers to the decrease in performance of LED fixtures when they are first turned on in cold temperatures. This is because LEDs are sensitive to temperature and take time to warm up to their optimal operating temperature. The RG-P58TC10-Ke18A, for example, has a cold-warm 2in1 white or RGB 3in1 LED configuration and can be affected by cold-start behaviour.

## How does winter derating affect LED fixtures?
Winter derating can cause LED fixtures to produce less light than expected, which can impact the overall lighting design. It's essential to consider this when planning a show or event in cold weather conditions. The RG-EL0001FL-3e844, a 7 Halo Hexagon LED Pixel Light, has a power consumption of 800W and can be affected by derating in winter.

## Can I use outdoor-rated fixtures in cold weather?
Yes, outdoor-rated fixtures like the RG-P58TC10-Ke18A are designed to withstand cold temperatures and can be used in winter conditions. However, it's still important to consider derating and plan accordingly. The RG-P58TC10-Ke18A has an IP rating of IP65, making it suitable for outdoor use in cold weather.

## What are the key considerations when choosing LED fixtures for winter use?
When choosing LED fixtures for winter use, consider the IP rating, derating factor, and warm-up time. It's also essential to check the specifications of the fixture, such as the voltage and power requirements, to ensure they can handle the cold temperatures. The RG-FR750H5-D8, a moving head cold spark machine, has a voltage range of AC110V/220V, 50/60HZ and can be used in winter conditions.

To plan a show around cold-start behaviour, consider using fixtures with a high IP rating, like IP65, and plan for a longer warm-up time.
