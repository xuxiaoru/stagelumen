## What is pixel mapping?
Pixel mapping is a technique used to control and display images or videos on a wall of LED fixtures. It involves assigning a unique address to each LED fixture, allowing for precise control over the color and brightness of each fixture. This technique is commonly used in various applications, including stage lighting, architectural lighting, and advertising displays.
