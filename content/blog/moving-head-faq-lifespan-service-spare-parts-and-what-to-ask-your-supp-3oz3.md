## What to Ask Your Supplier

When purchasing a moving head, there are several things you should ask your supplier. Here are some questions to consider:

* What is the lifespan of the moving head?
* What kind of service and support do you provide?
* Do you offer spare parts and replacement assemblies?
* What are the payment terms and delivery options?
* Do you offer any discounts for bulk orders?

## Choosing the Right Moving Head

When choosing a moving head, there are several factors to consider. Here are some things to think about:

* Beam angle: Consider the beam angle of the moving head. Do you need a narrow or wide beam?
* Color temperature: Consider the color temperature of the moving head. Do you need a warm or cool white light?
* Power consumption: Consider the power consumption of the moving head. Do you need a low or high power consumption?
* Control mode: Consider the control mode of the moving head. Do you need a self-walking, voice control, or DMX 512 control mode?

## Maintenance and Repair

Regular maintenance and repair are essential to extending the lifespan of your moving head. Here are some tips to keep in mind:

* Clean the moving head regularly to prevent dust and debris from accumulating.
* Check the moving head for any signs of wear or damage.
* Replace any worn or damaged parts promptly.
* Follow the manufacturer's instructions for maintenance and repair.
