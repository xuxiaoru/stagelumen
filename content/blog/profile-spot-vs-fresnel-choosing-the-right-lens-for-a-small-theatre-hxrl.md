Profile spot and fresnel lenses are two common types of lenses used in stage lighting. Both have their own unique characteristics and are suited for different applications. For a small theatre, choosing the right lens can be a crucial decision.

## What is a profile spot lens?
A profile spot lens is a type of lens that uses an elliptical reflector and a lens to create a sharp, defined beam of light. This type of lens is often used in theatrical applications where a precise beam of light is required.

## What is a fresnel lens?
A fresnel lens is a type of lens that uses a series of concentric rings to focus the light. This type of lens is often used in theatrical applications where a softer, more diffuse light is desired.

## What are the advantages of a profile spot lens?
The advantages of a profile spot lens include its ability to create a sharp, defined beam of light and its high level of precision. This makes it ideal for applications where a specific area of the stage needs to be highlighted.

## What are the disadvantages of a profile spot lens?
The disadvantages of a profile spot lens include its limited flexibility and its tendency to create harsh shadows. This can make it less suitable for applications where a softer, more diffuse light is desired.

## What are the advantages of a fresnel lens?
The advantages of a fresnel lens include its ability to create a softer, more diffuse light and its flexibility. This makes it ideal for applications where a more subtle lighting effect is desired.

## What are the disadvantages of a fresnel lens?
The disadvantages of a fresnel lens include its limited precision and its tendency to create a less defined beam of light. This can make it less suitable for applications where a sharp, defined beam is required.

## How do I choose between a profile spot and a fresnel lens?
To choose between a profile spot and a fresnel lens, consider the specific needs of your theatre. If a sharp, defined beam is required, a profile spot may be the better choice. If a softer, more diffuse light is desired, a fresnel lens may be more suitable.
