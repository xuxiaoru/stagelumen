## How often should I inspect my rigging hardware?

## What are the benefits of using a safety bond?
Using a safety bond can provide an additional layer of safety and security for your equipment and personnel. A safety bond is designed to catch and hold equipment in the event of a failure, preventing it from falling and causing damage or injury. By properly securing a safety bond, you can help prevent accidents and ensure a safe and successful show.

## How do I properly secure a safety bond?
To properly secure a safety bond, it's essential to follow the manufacturer's instructions and take into account the specific requirements of the equipment and venue. It's also crucial to regularly inspect the safety bond for any signs of wear or damage, and to replace it if necessary.
