LED Strobes and Audience Blinders for Live Music: A Buyer's Guide

Choose 600W strobes like RG-TH8B600S

October 6, 2026 · RiGeBa Lighting Team · How-To

LED strobes and audience blinders are essential components of live music events, creating a dynamic and engaging atmosphere for the audience. However, with so many options available, it can be difficult to choose the right fixtures for your specific needs.

The short answer

For live music events, consider 600W strobes like the RG-TH8B600S, which offers a high output and adjustable flash rate.

What is the purpose of LED strobes and audience blinders in live music?

LED strobes and audience blinders are used to create a dynamic and engaging atmosphere at live music events. They can be used to create a variety of effects, from subtle nuances to intense and dramatic flashes.

How do I choose the right output for my LED strobe or audience blinder?

The right output depends on the size of your venue and the type of event. For example, a 600W strobe like the RG-TH8B600S may be suitable for a large venue, while a smaller venue may require a lower output. Consider the size of your audience and the type of music being performed when selecting an output.

What is the importance of flash rate in LED strobes?

The flash rate of an LED strobe can greatly impact the overall effect of the lighting. A faster flash rate can create a more intense and dynamic effect, while a slower flash rate can create a more subtle and nuanced effect. Consider the type of music and the desired atmosphere when selecting a flash rate.

Can I use LED strobes and audience blinders outdoors?

Yes, some LED strobes and audience blinders are designed for outdoor use. For example, the RG-TH4B400-W4 is an outdoor LED blinder that can be used in a variety of weather conditions. Consider the environmental conditions and the type of event when selecting an outdoor fixture.

How do I control my LED strobes and audience blinders?

Most LED strobes and audience blinders can be controlled using DMX512, which allows for precise control over the lighting effects. Some models, like the RG-TH8B600S, also offer master-slave mode and auto working mode. Consider the level of control you need and the complexity of your lighting setup when selecting a control protocol.

What are the key considerations when choosing an LED strobe or audience blinder?

When choosing an LED strobe or audience blinder, consider the output, flash rate, control protocol, and environmental conditions. Also, consider the size and weight of the fixture, as well as the power consumption and cooling requirements.

How do I determine the right size and configuration for my LED strobe or audience blinder?

The right size and configuration depend on the specific needs of your event. Consider the size of your venue, the type of music, and the desired atmosphere. Also, consider the number of fixtures you need and the layout of your lighting setup.

There are several types of LED strobes and audience blinders available, including indoor and outdoor models, different output levels, and various control protocols. Consider the specific needs of your event and the type of fixture that best suits your needs.
