Beam angle and output are two critical factors in stage lighting that can greatly impact the overall effect of a performance. For technicians and lighting designers, understanding the relationship between these two factors is essential for creating a visually stunning and engaging show.

## What is beam angle and how does it affect output?
Beam angle refers to the width of the light beam, measured in degrees. A narrower beam angle results in a more focused light, while a wider beam angle produces a more dispersed light. The beam angle can greatly impact the output of the light, as a narrower beam angle can produce a brighter light even if the output is lower.

## How do I choose the right beam angle for my stage lighting needs?
Choosing the right beam angle depends on several factors, including the size of your stage, the distance between the light and the subject, and the desired effect. A narrower beam angle is suitable for longer throws, while a wider beam angle is better for shorter throws. For example, the RG-M230BN-KMH16 has a narrow beam angle, making it suitable for longer throws.
