## What is the purpose of ground support in stage platforms?
Ground support is crucial in stage platforms as it provides stability and security to the platform, ensuring that it can withstand the weight of the equipment, performers, and audience. A stable ground support system prevents accidents and damage to equipment, and it also helps to maintain the integrity of the platform.

## What are the key factors to consider when choosing a stage platform?
When choosing a stage platform, consider the size of your event, the type of performance, and the load capacity required. You should also consider the material and construction of the platform, as well as any additional features such as adjustable height or folding design. The TS-ST100100BFY-K60T100 Circular Stage, for example, has a diameter of 720mm and a height of 120mm, making it suitable for small to medium-sized events.

## How do I determine the load capacity of a stage platform?
To determine the load capacity of a stage platform, check the manufacturer's specifications and guidelines. The load capacity is usually measured in kilograms per square meter (kg/㎡), and it's essential to confirm this before load-in to ensure safety. The TS-ST100100BFY-K60T100 Circular Stage, for example, has a static load capacity of 200kg/㎡ and a dynamic load capacity of 100kg/㎡.

## What are the different types of stage platforms available?
There are various types of stage platforms available, including circular, rectangular, and folding platforms. Each type has its own unique features and benefits, and the choice of platform depends on the specific needs of the event. ## How do I set up ground support for my stage platform?
To set up ground support for your stage platform, ensure that the ground is level and secure, and that the platform is properly anchored to the ground. You should also follow the manufacturer's instructions and guidelines for setting up the platform, and ensure that the platform is stable and secure before load-in.

## What are the benefits of using a stage platform with adjustable height?
Using a stage platform with adjustable height provides flexibility and convenience, as it allows you to adjust the height of the platform to suit different performances and events. This feature is particularly useful for events that require multiple levels or heights, such as concerts or theatre productions.

## How do I ensure the safety of my stage platform during load-in and load-out?
To ensure the safety of your stage platform, always follow the manufacturer's instructions and guidelines. Make sure to inspect the platform before use, and ensure that it is properly secured and level. You should also have a trained crew to handle the load-in and load-out process, and ensure that the platform is stable and secure before use.

## What are the key features to consider when choosing a stage platform for outdoor events?
When choosing a stage platform for outdoor events, consider the weather resistance and durability of the platform, as well as its stability and security in windy or rainy conditions. You should also consider the size and weight of the platform, and ensure that it is easy to transport and set up.
