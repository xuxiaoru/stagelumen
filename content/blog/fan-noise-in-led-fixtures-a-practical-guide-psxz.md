## How do I measure fan noise in LED fixtures?
To measure fan noise in LED fixtures, you can use a sound level meter. This device can help you determine the decibel level of the fan noise. You can also check the specifications of the LED fixture for its noise level, usually measured in decibels.

## What is the acceptable fan noise level for different applications?
The acceptable fan noise level varies depending on the application. For example, in churches, the acceptable fan noise level is typically around 20-30 decibels. In theaters, it's around 30-40 decibels. In recording studios, it's usually below 20 decibels.
