## What is the importance of phase balance in stage lighting?
Phase balance is crucial in stage lighting to ensure that the power distribution is even and efficient. This helps to prevent overheating, reduces the risk of electrical shocks, and minimizes the strain on the electrical system. To achieve phase balance, you need to distribute the load evenly across the stage and ensure that the power sources are properly connected.

## Can I use a single power source for all my LED fixtures?
It's not recommended to use a single power source for all your LED fixtures, as this can lead to overload and electrical issues. Instead, consider using multiple power sources and distributing the load evenly across the stage. For example, you can use separate power sources for the [Full Moon Scimitar LED effect light](/products/pixel/full-moon-scimitar-led-effect) and the [6W Moving Head Full Color Laser](/products/laser/6w-moving-head-full-color).
