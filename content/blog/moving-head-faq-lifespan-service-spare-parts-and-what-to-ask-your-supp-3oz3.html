Moving Head FAQ: Lifespan, Service, Spare Parts and What to Ask Your Supplier

Get the most out of your moving head with our expert advice on lifespan, service, spare parts and what to ask your supplier.

September 20, 2026 · RiGeBa Lighting Team · How-To

Lifespan of Moving Heads

The lifespan of a moving head depends on various factors such as usage, maintenance, and quality of the product. Our moving heads are designed to last for a long time, with some models lasting up to 2200 hours.

For example, the New 295W 10R beam moving head light has a lifespan of 2200 hours. This means that if you use it for 4 hours a day, it will last for approximately 550 days.

Service and Support

At RiGeBa Lighting, we provide excellent service and support to our customers. We have a team of experts who are available to answer any questions or concerns you may have.

If you need technical support, you can email us with your product model, purchase reference, and a description of the issue. Include photos or a short video if the problem is visual, as this will speed up diagnosis considerably.

Spare Parts

We also provide spare parts and replacement assemblies for our moving heads. If you need a spare part, send us the model number and the part you need, and we will quote availability.

For example, if you need a spare part for the New 295W 10R beam moving head light, send us the model number and the part you need, and we will quote availability.

What to Ask Your Supplier

When purchasing a moving head, there are several things you should ask your supplier. Here are some questions to consider:

  • What is the lifespan of the moving head?
  • What kind of service and support do you provide?
  • Do you offer spare parts and replacement assemblies?
  • What are the payment terms and delivery options?
  • Do you offer any discounts for bulk orders?

Choosing the Right Moving Head

When choosing a moving head, there are several factors to consider. Here are some things to think about:

  • Beam angle: Consider the beam angle of the moving head. Do you need a narrow or wide beam?
  • Color temperature: Consider the color temperature of the moving head. Do you need a warm or cool white light?
  • Power consumption: Consider the power consumption of the moving head. Do you need a low or high power consumption?
  • Control mode: Consider the control mode of the moving head. Do you need a self-walking, voice control, or DMX 512 control mode?

Maintenance and Repair

Regular maintenance and repair are essential to extending the lifespan of your moving head. Here are some tips to keep in mind:

  • Clean the moving head regularly to prevent dust and debris from accumulating.
  • Check the moving head for any signs of wear or damage.
  • Replace any worn or damaged parts promptly.
  • Follow the manufacturer's instructions for maintenance and repair.

Warranty and Returns

At RiGeBa Lighting, we offer a warranty on our moving heads. If you need to return a moving head, please contact us and we will provide you with a return merchandise authorization (RMA) number.

Conclusion

In conclusion, choosing the right moving head for your needs requires careful consideration of several factors. By asking the right questions and doing your research, you can find the perfect moving head for your application.

If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us. We are here to help you find the perfect moving head for your needs.
