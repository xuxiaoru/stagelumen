Cold-start behaviour is a critical consideration for technicians and lighting designers working with LED fixtures, especially during winter months. When LED fixtures are first turned on in cold temperatures, they may not operate at full capacity, which can impact the overall lighting design. This derating can be a challenge for technicians who need to ensure that their lighting setup meets the required standards.

## The short answer
The 300W LED Panel light, with a color temperature of 3200K/5600K/2-in-1 optional, can be affected by cold-start behaviour, resulting in a reduced light output of up to 20% in extreme cold temperatures.

## What is cold-start behaviour?
Cold-start behaviour refers to the reduced performance of LED fixtures when they are first turned on in cold temperatures. This is because LEDs are sensitive to temperature and may not operate at full capacity until they have warmed up. The 300W LED Panel light, for example, has a voltage range of AC110-240V, 50-60HZ, which can be affected by cold temperatures.

## How does winter derating affect LED fixtures?
Winter derating can cause LED fixtures to produce less light than expected, which can impact the overall lighting design. The 24pcs 1.5W RGB Outdoor LED bar, with an EXW price of US$49, can be used in cold weather, but it's crucial to check the IP Rating line in the product's specification table to ensure it can withstand the conditions.

## What can be done to mitigate cold-start behaviour?
To mitigate cold-start behaviour, technicians can use fixtures with a high color rendering index, like the 300W LED Panel light, which has a color rendering index of Ra≥90. Additionally, ensuring that the lighting design takes into account the potential reduction in light output due to cold temperatures can help to minimize the impact of cold-start behaviour.
