## What is the difference between analog and TTL modulation?
Analog modulation and TTL modulation are two different methods of controlling the laser. Analog modulation uses a continuous signal to control the laser, while TTL modulation uses a digital signal. The choice between the two depends on the specific application and the level of control required.
