Step-by-Step Guide to Rigging and Aiming Moving Heads Safely

Check 6 heads full-color swinging laser

September 25, 2026 · RiGeBa Lighting Team · How-To

Introduction to Safe Rigging and Aiming

Rigging and aiming moving heads safely for a one-night event requires careful planning and execution. The first step is to assess the venue and determine the best placement for the moving heads. Consider the size of the stage, the type of event, and the desired lighting effects.

Pre-Event Checks

Before the event, conduct a thorough inspection of the moving heads, including the 6 heads full-color swinging laser. Check for any damage or wear and tear on the fixtures, cables, and rigging equipment. Ensure that all fixtures are properly secured to the truss or rigging system.

Rigging the Moving Heads

When rigging the moving heads, follow the manufacturer's instructions and guidelines. Use proper rigging techniques and equipment, such as clamps and safety cables, to secure the fixtures to the truss or rigging system. Ensure that the moving heads are properly balanced and aligned to prevent accidents or damage.

Aiming the Moving Heads

Once the moving heads are rigged, aim them according to the desired lighting design. Use a laser pointer or other aiming tool to ensure accurate placement. Consider using a fixture like the New 295W 10R beam moving head, which offers a high level of precision and control.

Focusing the Moving Heads

After aiming the moving heads, focus them to achieve the desired beam angle and shape. Use the fixture's built-in focus mechanisms or external focus tools to adjust the beam. For example, the 6W Moving head full color laser offers a beam range of X axis 540 degrees and Y axis 270 degrees.

Safety Protocols

Throughout the rigging and aiming process, follow strict safety protocols to prevent accidents or injuries. Ensure that all personnel are properly trained and equipped, and that the venue is clear of obstacles and hazards. Use safety equipment, such as harnesses and fall protection gear, when working at heights.

Testing and Quality Control

Before the event, test the moving heads to ensure they are functioning properly. Check for any issues with the fixtures, cables, or rigging equipment, and make any necessary adjustments. Use a fixture like the RG-ML80B-KCRe8AH38, which offers a reliable and efficient lighting solution.

Event Setup and Teardown

During the event setup and teardown, follow the same safety protocols and guidelines as during the rigging and aiming process. Ensure that all personnel are properly trained and equipped, and that the venue is clear of obstacles and hazards. Use safety equipment, such as harnesses and fall protection gear, when working at heights.

Final Checks

Before the event starts, conduct a final check of the moving heads and rigging equipment. Ensure that all fixtures are properly secured and aimed, and that the lighting design is achieved. Use a fixture like the RG-EL00DB-8e2A, which offers a high level of flexibility and control.

Next Steps

Proceed to the event setup and ensure that all moving heads are properly powered and controlled. Use a fixture like the RG-GB200L6C0-ZK, which offers a reliable and efficient lighting solution. Check the projection distance and angle to ensure optimal performance.
