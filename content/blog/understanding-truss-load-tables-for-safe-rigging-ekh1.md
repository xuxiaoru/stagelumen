## What is a truss load table?
A truss load table is a document provided by the manufacturer that outlines the weight capacity and configuration of a truss. It typically includes information about the truss size, weight capacity, and configuration, as well as any specific safety guidelines or recommendations.

## How do I read a truss load table?
To read a truss load table, you need to understand the different columns and rows, which typically include the truss size, weight capacity, and configuration. The table will usually provide information about the maximum weight capacity of the truss, as well as any specific safety guidelines or recommendations.

## What is the difference between uniformly distributed load and point load?
Uniformly distributed load refers to the weight that is evenly spread across the truss, while point load refers to a concentrated weight at a specific point. Understanding the difference is crucial for safe rigging, as overloading a truss can result in serious safety risks.

## How do I determine the right size and configuration for my truss?
To determine the right size and configuration for your truss, you need to consider the weight and size of the equipment you will be hanging, as well as the venue's structural limitations. Consulting the manufacturer's specifications and seeking professional advice is recommended.

Overloading a truss can result in serious safety risks, including collapse and injury. It's crucial to follow the manufacturer's specifications and guidelines to ensure safe rigging.
