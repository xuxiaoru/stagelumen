Moving heads are a crucial part of any lighting system, and regular maintenance is essential to ensure they continue to function properly. For technicians responsible for maintaining moving heads, it is essential to follow a routine maintenance schedule to prevent damage and ensure optimal performance.

## The short answer
Clean your moving heads every 2200 hours, and replace the belt when it shows signs of wear. Store the fixtures in a dry, cool place, away from direct sunlight.

## What is the importance of regular maintenance?
Regular maintenance is crucial to ensure the moving heads continue to function properly and prevent damage. Neglecting maintenance can lead to reduced performance, malfunction, or even complete failure of the moving head.

## How often should I clean my moving heads?
It is recommended to clean your moving heads every 2200 hours to ensure optimal performance and prevent damage. Use a soft cloth and avoid harsh chemicals. Refer to the product manual for specific cleaning instructions.

## What are the signs of a worn-out belt?
A worn-out belt can cause the moving head to malfunction or not move at all. Signs of a worn-out belt include cracks, frays, or excessive wear on the belt. Replace the belt immediately if you notice any of these signs.

## How do I replace the belt?
To replace the belt, follow the manufacturer's instructions and use the correct replacement belt. Make sure to align the belt properly and tighten it securely to prevent slipping or malfunction.

## What are the different types of moving heads?
There are various types of moving heads, including beam moving heads, such as the [New 295W 10R beam moving head](/products/moving/new-295w-10r-beam-moving), and full-color laser moving heads, such as the [6W Moving head full color laser](/products/laser/6w-moving-head-full-color).

## How do I store my moving heads between tours?
Store your moving heads in a dry, cool place, away from direct sunlight. Use a protective case or cover to prevent damage and dust accumulation. Make sure the moving heads are completely dry before storing them to prevent moisture damage.

## How do I check the moving head's electrical connections?
Check the moving head's electrical connections regularly, ideally every 100 hours of use, to ensure they are secure and not damaged. Loose or damaged connections can cause the moving head to malfunction or not work at all.

## How do I troubleshoot common issues with my moving heads?
To troubleshoot common issues with your moving heads, refer to the product manual or consult a professional technician. Common issues include malfunctioning motors, faulty electrical connections, or damaged optics.

## How do I prevent dust accumulation in my moving heads?
To prevent dust accumulation in your moving heads, store them in a dry, cool place, away from direct sunlight. Use a protective case or cover to prevent dust accumulation, and clean the moving heads regularly to prevent damage.
