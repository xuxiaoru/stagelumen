## What is the typical panel pitch for LED dance floors?
The typical panel pitch for LED dance floors can range from 2.976mm to 3.91mm, depending on the model and manufacturer. For example, the RG-VDF44S2976-KV0 has a panel pitch of 2.976mm, while the RG-VDF44S391-KV0 has a panel pitch of 3.91mm. A smaller panel pitch generally provides a higher resolution and more detailed visuals, but may also increase the cost.

## How do I determine the right size and configuration for my LED dance floor?
To determine the right size and configuration for your LED dance floor, consider the space where it will be installed, the intended use, and the desired visual effect. You may also want to consult with a professional to ensure that the floor is properly integrated with your existing lighting and sound systems. The size and configuration of the LED dance floor will impact the overall visual experience and the level of engagement with the audience.

## What is the weight loading capacity of LED dance floors?
The weight loading capacity of LED dance floors can vary depending on the model and manufacturer. For example, the RG-IDF17S305-156 has a weight loading capacity of 380KG㎡ (Resin panel bearing) or 500KG㎡ (Glass panel bearing). It's essential to consider the weight loading capacity to ensure that the floor can support the intended use and avoid any damage or safety risks.

## How is the DMX mapped for LED dance floors?
The DMX mapping for LED dance floors can vary depending on the model and manufacturer. However, most LED dance floors use a standard DMX protocol, such as DMX512, to control the lighting effects and patterns. It's essential to consult the user manual or manufacturer's documentation to understand the specific DMX mapping for your LED dance floor. The DMX mapping will impact the level of control and customization you have over the lighting effects.
