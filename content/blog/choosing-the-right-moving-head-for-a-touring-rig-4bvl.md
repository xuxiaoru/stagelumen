## Understanding the Requirements of a Touring Rig

When it comes to choosing a moving head for a touring rig, there are several key factors to consider. The first is the wattage of the fixture. A higher wattage fixture will provide more light output, but it will also consume more power and may be more expensive.

## Wattage Considerations

For a touring rig, a 400W or 600W fixture is often a good choice. These wattages offer a good balance between light output and power consumption. The SL-B400 and SL-B600 are two examples of fixtures that fit within this wattage range.

## Beam Angle Considerations

The beam angle of a moving head is also an important consideration. A narrower beam angle will provide a more focused beam of light, while a wider beam angle will provide a more diffuse beam. For a touring rig, a beam angle of around 10-20 degrees is often a good choice.

## Control Protocol Considerations

The control protocol of a moving head is also an important consideration. DMX512 is a widely used protocol that allows for precise control of the fixture's movements and color changes. RDM (Remote Device Management) is also a useful protocol that allows for remote monitoring and control of the fixture.

## Practical Trade-Offs

When choosing a moving head for a touring rig, there are several practical trade-offs to consider. For example, a fixture with a higher wattage and narrower beam angle may be more expensive and consume more power, but it will also provide more light output. On the other hand, a fixture with a lower wattage and wider beam angle may be less expensive and consume less power, but it will also provide less light output.

## Selecting the Right Fixture

To select the right fixture for a touring rig, it is essential to consider the specific requirements of the show. This includes the wattage, beam angle, and control protocol of the fixture. By considering these factors and making a practical trade-off, it is possible to choose a moving head that meets the needs of the show while also being cost-effective and energy-efficient.
