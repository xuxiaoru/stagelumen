Decoding Moving Head Spec Sheets

How to read a moving head spec sheet: output, beam angle, pan and tilt range, IP rating and the numbers that actually decide a purchase.

September 27, 2026 · RiGeBa Lighting Team · How-To

Introduction to Moving Head Spec Sheets

When it comes to stage lighting, moving heads are a crucial component. To choose the right one, technicians need to understand the specifications provided by manufacturers. This tutorial will guide you through the process of reading a moving head spec sheet, focusing on key aspects such as pan/tilt, zoom, and CRI.

Understanding Pan/Tilt

Pan and tilt refer to the movement of the moving head in horizontal and vertical directions, respectively. These values are usually measured in degrees. For instance, a moving head with a pan range of 540 degrees and a tilt range of 270 degrees offers a wide range of movement. The 6W Moving head full color laser, for example, has a pan range of 540 degrees and a tilt range of 270 degrees, making it suitable for various applications.

Zoom Functionality

The zoom function allows the moving head to adjust its beam angle, providing flexibility in lighting design. A moving head with a zoom feature can be used to create both narrow and wide beams. The RG-ML760WC-KCH99, with its 19x40W LED configuration, features a zoom function, enabling technicians to adjust the beam angle according to their needs.

Color Rendering Index (CRI)

CRI is a measure of how well a light source renders the colors of an object. A higher CRI value indicates better color rendering. While CRI is not always specified in moving head spec sheets, it is an essential factor to consider, especially in applications where color accuracy is crucial.

Checking the Specifications

When reading a moving head spec sheet, it's essential to check the specifications carefully. Look for values such as life (e.g., 2200 hours for the New 295W 10R beam moving head), voltage (e.g., 110v/220v for the 6W Moving head full color laser), and laser power (e.g., 6W full-color laser for the 6W Moving head full color laser). These values will help you determine if the moving head is suitable for your specific application.

Considering the Application

Different moving heads are designed for various applications. For instance, the 6 heads full-color swinging laser is suitable for bars, music restaurants, and KTVs, while the RG-ML760WC-KCH99 is designed for outdoor use with its IP65 rating. Consider the specific requirements of your application and choose a moving head that meets those needs.

Volume Pricing and Discounts

When purchasing moving heads, it's essential to consider the pricing and discounts offered by the manufacturer. The RG-ML500BL-KCRe12AH194, for example, has a volume price of $290 for orders over 50 pieces. Understanding the pricing structure can help you make informed decisions about your lighting equipment.

Next Steps

Now that you have a better understanding of moving head spec sheets, it's time to apply this knowledge to your specific needs. Start by identifying the key specifications required for your application, such as pan/tilt range, zoom functionality, and CRI. Then, research different moving head models, such as the New 295W 10R beam moving head or the RG-ML760WC-KCH99, and compare their specifications to your requirements. By doing so, you'll be able to select the most suitable moving head for your stage lighting needs.
