## The short answer

## What are the key considerations when choosing a lighting package?
When choosing a lighting package, there are several key considerations to keep in mind. These include the type of events or productions the rental company will be catering to, the size of the venues, and the budget. It is also essential to consider the durability and reliability of the fixtures, as well as their ease of use and maintenance.

## What is the difference between beam and wash lights?
Beam lights, such as the RG-M300BH-KYH16 model, are designed to produce a narrow beam of light, typically with a beam angle of 10-30 degrees. They are often used to highlight specific areas or objects on stage. Wash lights, on the other hand, produce a wider beam of light, typically with a beam angle of 30-60 degrees, and are used to illuminate larger areas.

## How do I determine the right size and configuration for my lighting package?
To determine the right size and configuration for a lighting package, it is essential to consider the size of the venues and the type of events or productions the rental company will be catering to. A larger venue will require more fixtures, while a smaller venue may require fewer. It is also essential to consider the power requirements of the fixtures and ensure that the package includes a sufficient number of power supplies.

## What are the benefits of using LED fixtures?
LED fixtures, such as the RG-ML300BR-KNNW1H25 model, offer several benefits, including energy efficiency, long lifespan, and low maintenance. They are also often more durable and reliable than traditional fixtures and can withstand the rigors of frequent use.

The IP rating of an outdoor lighting fixture, such as the RG-M230B-KOH20 model, is essential in determining its suitability for use in different weather conditions.
