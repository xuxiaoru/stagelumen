DMX splitters and opto-isolation are essential components in any DMX setup, especially when one universe needs to support multiple cable runs. For technicians and engineers working with DMX systems, understanding how to properly configure and use DMX splitters is crucial for ensuring reliable and efficient operation.

## The short answer
To support four cable runs from one DMX universe, you can use a DMX splitter like the RG-CA8402C, which supports up to 4 outputs and has a voltage requirement of AC90V-240V.

## What is DMX opto-isolation?
DMX opto-isolation is a method of isolating DMX signals to prevent interference and damage to equipment. It uses optical signals to transmit data, rather than electrical signals. This is especially important in setups where multiple devices are connected to a single universe, as it helps to prevent signal degradation and ensures reliable operation.

## How many cable runs can one DMX universe support?
One DMX universe can support up to 32 devices, but the number of cable runs required depends on the specific setup and equipment used. In this case, we need four cable runs, which can be supported by a single DMX universe using a DMX splitter.

## What is the purpose of a DMX splitter?
A DMX splitter is used to split a single DMX signal into multiple signals, allowing multiple devices to be controlled from a single universe. This is especially useful in setups where multiple devices need to be controlled from a single console or controller.

## How do I choose the right DMX splitter for my setup?
To choose the right DMX splitter, consider the number of devices you need to control, the distance between devices, and the type of equipment you are using. You should also consider the specifications of the splitter, such as the voltage and power requirements. For example, the RG-CA8402C has a voltage requirement of AC90V-240V and supports up to 4 outputs.
