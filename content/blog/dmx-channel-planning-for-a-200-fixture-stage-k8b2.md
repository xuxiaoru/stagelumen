# Choosing the Right Fixture Type

When planning DMX channels for a 200-fixture stage, the first step is to choose the right fixture type. This decision will impact the number of DMX channels required and the overall complexity of the lighting system.

## Considerations for Fixture Selection

When selecting fixtures, consider the following factors:

*   **Wattage**: Higher-wattage fixtures require more DMX channels to control their intensity and color.
*   **Beam Angle**: Fixtures with adjustable beam angles require more DMX channels to control the zoom and focus.
*   **IP Rating**: Outdoor-rated fixtures require more DMX channels to control their IP rating and environmental settings.
*   **Control Protocol**: Fixtures with RDM (Remote Device Management) require more DMX channels to control their settings and parameters.

## Fixture Types and DMX Channel Requirements

Here are some common fixture types and their DMX channel requirements:

*   **LED Moving Heads**: These fixtures require 16-24 DMX channels to control their movement, color, and intensity.
*   **Profile Spots**: These fixtures require 1-3 DMX channels to control their focus, color, and intensity.
*   **LED Washes**: These fixtures require 16-24 DMX channels to control their movement, color, and intensity.

## Planning DMX Channels

To plan DMX channels for a 200-fixture stage, follow these steps:

1.  **Determine the number of fixtures**: Calculate the total number of fixtures required for the stage.
2.  **Choose the right fixture type**: Select the fixture type that best suits the application and requirements.
3.  **Determine the DMX channel requirements**: Calculate the number of DMX channels required for each fixture type.
4.  **Plan the DMX channel layout**: Create a layout that minimizes the number of DMX channels required and ensures easy access to each fixture.

## Example DMX Channel Layout

Here's an example DMX channel layout for a 200-fixture stage:

*   **Fixture Type**: LED Moving Heads (16-24 DMX channels each)
*   **Number of Fixtures**: 100
*   **Total DMX Channels**: 1600-2400
*   **DMX Channel Layout**: Divide the DMX channels into 4 groups, each controlling a quadrant of the stage.

## Conclusion

Planning DMX channels for a 200-fixture stage requires careful consideration of the fixture type, wattage, beam angle, IP rating, and control protocol. By following these steps and considering the DMX channel requirements for each fixture type, you can create an efficient and effective DMX channel layout for your stage.
