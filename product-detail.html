Tour-tested beam lighting, OEM-ready

The SL-B230 is our flagship 230W Beam Moving Head, designed for concerts, clubs and rental companies who need punchy aerial effects without the weight penalty. The 1.5° beam cuts through haze crisply and pans 540° in under 2 seconds.

Manufactured in our 8,000 m² facility in Guangzhou with 100% 48-hour aging tests, every unit ships with individual QC report and IES files for venue designers.

  • 1.5° ultra-narrow beam, 100,000 lux @ 10m
  • 14 color wheel + 0–100% dimming + 1–20Hz strobe
  • Auto-run, sound-active, master/slave, DMX modes
  • 3-pin / 5-pin XLR in/out, power in/out (daisy chain 6 units)
  • Optional frost filter + motorised focus
