Our Process

Build Your Own Lighting Product Line

Looking for more than standard products? We work with distributors, rental companies, lighting brands and professional buyers on OEM and ODM projects.

01

Requirements

Define your target market, application, specifications and product positioning.

02

Engineering

Develop optical, mechanical, electronic and control solutions around your requirements.

03

Prototype

Build and evaluate samples before production.

04

Testing

Verify performance, functionality and production requirements.

05

Approval

What We Can Customize

OEM / ODM Capability

Don't just put your logo on a box. Build a product that fits your market.

Branding

  • Logo on housing and user interface
  • Custom housing finish and colors
  • Label, packaging and manuals

Optical

  • LED source selection
  • Lens and beam angle options
  • Color system and gobo customization

Control

Mechanical

  • Cooling system optimization
