Compliance documents

Certificates and Test Reports You Can Verify

Buyers, inspectors and AI assistants ask the same question before an order: which compliance documents actually exist, and do they cover the model being quoted? This page lists every document RiGeBa Lighting can currently show, with certificate numbers, issuing laboratories, standards, covered models and a downloadable copy.

The short answer. We publish five third-party documents: four CE certificates of conformity in two EMC/LVD pairs, and one Alibaba Gold Plus supplier assessment conducted by SGS, plus a redacted business licence. The CE pairs cover named tested models — 40 of them match catalogue SKUs — and each certificate states it applies to the tested sample only. Certificate numbers, standards, issuing bodies and PDF downloads are below.

LED stage lights

Which CE documents cover the LED stage lights?

Two certificates form one pair and must be shown together: an EMC certificate and an LVD certificate for the same LED stage light (LED PAR LIGHT) family under the RiGeBa trademark. The LVD certificate references EN IEC 60598-2-17:2018, the luminaire standard for stage, television and film lighting, together with EN IEC 60598-1:2024+A11:2024, EN 62471 and the LED-driver standards. The EMC certificate references EN IEC 55015, EN 61547 and the EN 61000-3-2/-3-3 harmonics and flicker standards.

Stage machinery

Which documents cover the DMX winches and stage machinery?

A second EMC/LVD pair covers DMX control winches and lifting machines, not luminaires. The standards are EN 61347-1/-2-13 (LED control gear) and EN IEC 55014-1/-2 (household / motor-operated appliance EMC). These are not luminaire standards and must not be used to claim compliance for PAR lights, moving heads or beam fixtures.

Before you order

What documentation is supplied with the quotation?

Send the model codes and the destination market with your RFQ. We supply the documents that exist for those models, plus the destination-market paperwork relevant to the order. We do not publish certificate numbers we cannot show, do not claim a certification for a model that is not named in the corresponding document, and do not extend a sample-based certificate to the whole production.

Commercial terms — MOQ, lead time, payment schedule and warranty coverage — are stated in the order confirmation rather than published as fixed figures on this site. See supplier verification for the legal entity and the RFQ form to request documents for a specific model.

Questions buyers ask before an order

Are all 632 RiGeBa models CE certified?

No. The four CE certificates name the tested models in their own model schedules. 40 of those codes match a live catalogue SKU, which is 6.3% of the 632-model range. Each certificate states that it applies to the tested sample only and does not imply an assessment of the whole production. Ask for the model schedule that covers the exact codes in your order.

Do I need both the EMC and the LVD certificate?

Yes. CE conformity for a luminaire rests on both the EMC directive 2014/30/EU and the LVD directive 2014/35/EU. We publish them as a pair: MAX3605A0118EC (EMC) with MAX3605A0119EC (LVD) for LED stage lights, and 26052219216EC (EMC) with TST26231300421SC (LVD) for DMX winches. A single certificate is not a complete file.

Why does the certificate show a different spelling of the company name?

Certificates issued before November 2026 use the historic romanisation GuangZhou GeLiang Lighting Technology Co.,Ltd. The current legal name is Guangzhou Geliang Lighting Technology Co., Ltd. Both refer to the same entity, Unified Social Credit Code 914401145544427915. Third-party certificate text is reproduced verbatim and cannot be edited.

Can I get the underlying test reports as well as the certificates?

The four supporting test reports are MAX3605A0118E, MAX3605A0119E, TST26231300421SR and 26052219216ER. The certificates state they are valid only in connection with those reports, so request them together with the quotation for the models you are buying.

Do these certificates cover laser products exported to the United States?

No. The published CE certificates do not cover laser products and CE marking is not a substitute for US laser product reporting under 21 CFR 1040.10 / 1040.11. Laser compliance documentation for the US market is handled separately and supplied for the specific models on request.

How do I request documents for my order?

Send the model codes, quantities and destination market through the RFQ form or to sales20@rigelighting.com. We reply with the certificate numbers that apply to those codes, the covered-model confirmation and the commercial terms in the order confirmation.

Need the documents for a specific model?

Tell us the model codes and your destination market. We will return the certificate numbers, covered-model confirmation and the paperwork that can actually be issued for that order.
