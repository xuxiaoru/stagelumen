07 / BUYER SUPPORT

GOOD QUESTIONS.
STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

Need a specification, a sample or a tailored quotation? Start with the questions buyers ask most.

Visit buyer support ↗
Do you have a minimum order quantity?＋

MOQ is confirmed per model in the order confirmation. Send the model and quantity with your RFQ and we will state the applicable MOQ in the quotation.

Can I order samples before committing to volume?＋

Yes. Sample units are charged at the single-unit price plus freight. We can provide the aging-test result and packing photos for your review.

Can you print my logo and use my packaging?＋

Yes. Logo printing, housing colours, product naming, carton artwork, manuals and firmware behaviour can be discussed as part of OEM / ODM service.

What payment and shipping terms do you work with?＋

Prices are quoted EXW Guangzhou. FOB, CIF and DDP shipping can be arranged to your port or door; payment terms are stated in the order confirmation.

What is your production lead time?＋

Lead time is stated in the order confirmation. Share the model, quantity and destination and we will confirm the production schedule in the quotation.
