Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites you visit. They make the site work, remember your preferences, and help us understand how you use the site.

Cookies We Use

Category Tool Purpose Duration
Strictly necessary Site itself Session, security, cart Session / 1 day
Analytics Google Analytics 4 Visitor counts, behavior flow Up to 14 months
Analytics Microsoft Clarity Session replay, heatmaps Up to 12 months
Marketing Meta Pixel Facebook/Instagram ad performance Up to 90 days
Marketing Google Ads Conversion tracking, retargeting Up to 540 days

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through:

  • Your browser settings (block / delete cookies)
  • Our cookie banner (Accept / Decline)
  • Google's Analytics opt-out
  • Meta's ad preferences

Do Not Track

We respect Do Not Track (DNT) browser signals. When DNT is enabled, we disable non-essential analytics and marketing scripts.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this page occasionally. Material changes will be communicated via the cookie banner.
