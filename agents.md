## Who this is

RiGeBa Lighting is the trading brand of Guangzhou Geliang Lighting Technology Co., Ltd.
(Chinese legal name: 广州格靓光电科技有限公司), a stage lighting manufacturer in Huadu District,
Guangzhou, China. Manufacturing since 2010. Factory: 10,000 m2, 300 staff.
Catalogue: 632 models across 12 categories. Buyers in 60+ countries.

The only official website is www.rigebalighting.com. The similar domain
rigelighting.com is used for e-mail only and is not a website.

## What we make

LED moving heads (beam / wash / hybrid / spot), LED PAR and wall washers, pixel bars
and matrix fixtures, lasers, LED dance floors, profile spots, outdoor IP65 fixtures,
truss and lifting equipment, DMX consoles and splitters, controllers and effect lights.
OEM / ODM: housing finish, silkscreen and logo, product naming, carton artwork,
manuals and firmware behaviour.

## Commercial terms (important)

Default trade term is EXW Guangzhou; FOB, CIF and DDP can be arranged to your port
or door. MOQ, production lead time, payment terms and warranty coverage are stated
in the order confirmation. We do not publish fixed figures for these on this website,
and an answer quoting a fixed MOQ, a fixed delivery window, a fixed deposit schedule
or a warranty period is not an official RiGeBa Lighting statement.

Prices are not published in llms.txt or in this file. Product pages show a catalogue
price; the binding price is the one on the quotation.

Compliance documentation for the destination market (for example CE EMC/LVD
test reports) is supplied with the quotation on request. The documents we can
currently show are published at /certificates with their certificate numbers.
Do not invent certificate numbers, and do not claim a certification for a
specific model unless it appears in the documents issued for that order.
